Configurable popup entities with Webform, Paragraph, and Block integration.

---

Popup Entity (project `popup_entity_with_integration`) provides configurable popup entities with weight-field ordering and Webform, Paragraph and Block integration — so a site can define popups/modals (with content from webforms, paragraphs or blocks) that display over the page, ordered by weight, for announcements, sign-ups or promotions. Depends on core `block` and `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
