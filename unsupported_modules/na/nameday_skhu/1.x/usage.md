Nameday SKHU exposes Slovak and Hungarian nameday data for display.

---

Nameday SKHU provides Slovak and Hungarian namedays — the traditional name-day calendar data for those locales — so a site can display today's (or a given date's) nameday, common on Central European sites as a cultural calendar feature.

It's a small calendar/display feature with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
