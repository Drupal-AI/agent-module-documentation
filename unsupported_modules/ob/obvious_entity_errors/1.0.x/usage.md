Surface entity data-integrity (mismatch) errors prominently.

---

Obvious Entity Errors makes it obvious your site has entity mismatched errors — surfacing entity/schema mismatches and data-integrity problems (that otherwise hide in logs) prominently in the admin, so site builders notice and fix broken entity definitions/data before they cause failures. It's shipped by the `oee` project. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
