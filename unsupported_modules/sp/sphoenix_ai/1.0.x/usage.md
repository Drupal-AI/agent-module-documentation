AI-powered content generation/analysis with a chatbot interface.

---

SPhoenix AI Content Assistant provides AI-powered content generation and analysis tools with a chatbot interface — connecting Drupal to the SPhoenix AI service so editors can generate/analyse content and interact with an in-admin chatbot assistant.

The SPhoenix AI credentials/authentication are admin-configured (the auth endpoints only return a redirect URL and status, exposing no secrets); store credentials securely (env-backed). Depends on core `node`, `field`, `user`, and `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
