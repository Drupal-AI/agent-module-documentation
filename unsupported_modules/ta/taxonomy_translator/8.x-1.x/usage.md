Bulk Taxonomy Term Translator translates many taxonomy terms into a language at once.

---

Bulk Taxonomy Term Translator provides mass translation of taxonomy terms into a new language — so instead of translating each term individually, editors can bulk-create translations for an entire vocabulary, speeding up multilingual taxonomy setup.

It's a multilingual/editorial tool; translation still follows core content-translation access. Supports Drupal 8.8+ through 11.
