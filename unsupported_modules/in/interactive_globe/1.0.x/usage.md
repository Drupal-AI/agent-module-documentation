An interactive 3D globe block with custom images and text.

---

Interactive Globe provides an interactive globe block with custom images and text — a rotatable 3D globe (rendered in the browser) that a site can place as a block, with configurable imagery and text markers/labels, for visual/geographic storytelling. It's shipped by the `iglobeblock` project. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
