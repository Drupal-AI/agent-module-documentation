Let certain roles override the site name, logo and colour scheme.

---

White Label provides white-label functionality by allowing certain roles to override the default site name, logo and colour scheme — so (e.g. in a multi-tenant or reseller setup) specific users/roles can rebrand the site's identity to their own, without separate installs. Depends on core `image`, `options`, and `entity_reference_revisions`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
