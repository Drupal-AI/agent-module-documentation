Block abusive words in Webform submissions (incl. leetspeak/homoglyph evasions).

---

Webform Leet Speak Filter blocks abusive words in Webform submissions including leet speak, homoglyphs, and character-substitution evasions — so a webform rejects profanity/abuse even when written as 'l33t' or with look-alike Unicode characters, with per-language word lists for multilingual sites. Depends on core `webform` and `language`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11. Project `wlsf`.
