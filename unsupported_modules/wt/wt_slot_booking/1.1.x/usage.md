The hourly appointment/service booking engine of the WT Commerce Suite.

---

WT Slot Booking is the hourly appointment and service booking engine for the WT Commerce Suite — letting a site offer time-slot bookings of services/appointments (per-hour), with availability and payment via WT Simple Pay, for salons, clinics and service providers. Depends on core `node` and `wt_simple_pay`; supports Drupal 11. Project `wt_commerce`.
