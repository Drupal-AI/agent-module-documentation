The daily hotel/room booking engine of the WT Commerce Suite.

---

WT Simple Booking is the daily hotel and room booking engine for the WT Commerce Suite — letting a site offer date-range (per-night) bookings of rooms/units, with availability and payment via WT Simple Pay, for hotel/accommodation sites. Depends on core `node` and `wt_simple_pay`; supports Drupal 11. Project `wt_commerce`.
