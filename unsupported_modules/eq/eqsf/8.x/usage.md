A field to schedule entities into/out of entity queues.

---

Entity Queue Scheduler Field (eqsf) provides an entity queue scheduler field — a field where editors set when an entity should be added to or removed from an Entityqueue, so queue membership (e.g. featured/promoted content) can be scheduled in advance rather than managed manually. Depends on `entityqueue`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
