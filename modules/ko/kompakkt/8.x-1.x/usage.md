A field to embed Kompakkt 3D/media viewers.

---

Kompakkt creates a field to embed Kompakkt — an open-source 3D/media viewer and annotation platform — so editors can embed interactive 3D models (and annotated media) from Kompakkt on content, useful for cultural-heritage and educational sites. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
