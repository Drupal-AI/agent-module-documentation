Kordiam connects Drupal to the Kordiam editorial-planning platform.

---

Kordiam integrates the Kordiam editorial-planning/newsroom platform with Drupal — connecting Drupal content with Kordiam's planning, scheduling and task management via its API, so newsrooms can align published content with their editorial plan.

Kordiam API access is gated by `access to Kordiam API` and credentials should be stored securely (env-backed). Depends on `pathauto` and core `path`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
