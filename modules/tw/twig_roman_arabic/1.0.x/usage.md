Twig Roman Arabic converts between Roman and Arabic numerals in Twig templates.

---

Twig Roman Arabic **converts Roman↔Arabic numerals in Twig** — Twig filters/functions to convert Roman
numerals to Arabic and vice versa in templates. It works across core 9–11.

Use it for numeral conversion in theming. It is a theming/developer feature; it transforms values in templates and
has no content or access role. Use the filters in Twig.
