Play legacy SWF/Flash content via the Ruffle emulator.

---

Ruffle integrates the Ruffle Flash Emulator to display SWF content — so legacy Adobe Flash `.swf` files can still be played in modern browsers (which dropped native Flash) by rendering them through the WebAssembly-based Ruffle player embedded on the page. Depends on core `file`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
