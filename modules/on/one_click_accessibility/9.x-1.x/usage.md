One Click Accessibility adds an accessibility toolbar offering common helpers like font sizing, contrast, links highlighting and more.

---

One Click Accessibility adds a front-end accessibility toolbar/widget to the site, giving visitors
common accessibility helpers — adjusting font size, contrast/greyscale, highlighting links, a readable
font, and similar aids — from a single control. It is configured at `one_click_accessibility.settings`.

Use it to improve on-page accessibility ergonomics for users who benefit from these adjustments. It is
a front-end/accessibility feature operating in the browser; it does not change content or access.
Note that a client-side toolbar is a usability aid and complements, rather than replaces, building
genuinely accessible markup (semantic HTML, alt text, ARIA) — it is not a substitute for WCAG-compliant
content.
