Migrate Forward Draft keeps forward (pending) draft revisions intact during migration.

---

Migrate Forward Draft provides a migrate destination plugin that preserves forward draft revisions when migrating content — so pending/forward revisions (drafts newer than the published version, used with content moderation) survive the migration instead of being flattened. It's a developer/migration tool.

It's a migration utility with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `migrate`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
