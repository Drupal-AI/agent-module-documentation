Migrate Skip Fields lets specific fields be skipped from being migrated as part of an update/migration.

---

During a migration or update, some fields should not carry over (deprecated fields, fields handled separately). Migrate Skip Fields lets those be excluded. It is developer/migration tooling with no request-time security surface. Confirm the skipped fields are intentional (skipping a field means its data is not migrated).
