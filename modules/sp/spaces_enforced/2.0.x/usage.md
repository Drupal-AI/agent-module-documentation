Require a space in usernames to deter no-space spam registrations.

---

Spaces Enforced! enforces spaces in usernames against no-space spammers — requiring new usernames to contain a space, a simple heuristic that blocks the common spam-bot pattern of run-together names, reducing spam registrations. Supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
