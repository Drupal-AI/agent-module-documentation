Simple Petitions provides a module to create and manage petitions.

---

Simple Petitions (spn) lets users **create and manage petitions** — building petition content that people
can sign, with listing/management via Views. It depends on a range of core modules (Link, Views, Block, Node,
Field, Path, User), in the bluedrop package.

Use it to run petitions on the site. It is a content/user-engagement feature. Privacy/security notes: petition
**signatures are personal data** (names, possibly emails) — store/expose them per your privacy policy, apply
**spam/bot protection** (CAPTCHA/honeypot) to signing forms to prevent fake signatures, and ensure signature
data isn't exposed beyond intent. Its access follows normal content/entity access. Configure petitions and
signing.
