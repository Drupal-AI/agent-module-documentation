Embed aggregated Rater8 reviews and ratings in a block.

---

Rater8 Reviews Block provides aggregated feedback and ratings from users or customers on various aspects of a service or product — embedding the Rater8 reviews widget in a block so a site can display its Rater8-collected star ratings and review summaries. The Rater8 widget id is a public identifier (the summary is loaded client-side from Rater8's public API). Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
