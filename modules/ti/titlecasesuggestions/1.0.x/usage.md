Auto-apply recommended title casing to article titles.

---

Title Case Suggestions sets article titles to have recommended title casing when an article title is filled in — automatically capitalising the title according to title-case rules as the editor types/leaves the field, for consistent, correctly-cased headlines. Supports Drupal 9.2+, 10, and 11.
