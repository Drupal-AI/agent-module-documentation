A progress bar showing whether a title length is accessibility-optimal.

---

Accessibility Indicator for Title Length generates a progress bar to indicate whether the title length falls within optimal ranges for accessibility — an editorial aid on the node title field that turns green/amber/red as the title grows, guiding editors toward accessible, appropriately-sized titles. Depends on core `node`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
