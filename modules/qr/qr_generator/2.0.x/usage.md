QR Code Generator provides tools to create QR codes, including self-hosted and offline (no third-party service) generation.

---

QR Code Generator provides tools to create QR codes in Drupal, with an emphasis on self-hosted and
offline generation — producing the codes on the server rather than calling an external QR service, so
no data is sent to a third party and it works without internet access. It depends on core File and REST
and provides its own permissions.

Use it to generate QR codes for URLs, content links, tickets or campaigns, kept entirely on your
infrastructure. Self-hosted generation is the privacy-friendly choice (nothing about the encoded data
leaves the server). It is a content/utility feature producing QR images; access to generation is
governed by its permissions. Ensure whatever is encoded (and any endpoint that generates codes from
input) is validated as normal.
