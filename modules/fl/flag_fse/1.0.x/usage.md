Flag for someone else lets a user apply a flag to content for another user.

---

Flag for someone else extends the Flag module with the ability to flag content on behalf of another user — for example an admin or moderator flagging/bookmarking an item for a specific member. It adds a link to perform the flag action for a chosen user.

Because it acts on behalf of others, per-flag permissions (via permission_callbacks) control who may flag for someone else — restrict to trusted roles. Depends on `flag`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
