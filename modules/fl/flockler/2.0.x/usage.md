Displays a Flockler.com social-media feed in a block.

---

Flockler feed displays a feed from flockler.com in a block — embedding a Flockler social-media aggregation feed (curated posts from social channels) on the site, so a site can show an on-brand social wall without building its own aggregation. The Flockler site/feed id is admin-configured. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
