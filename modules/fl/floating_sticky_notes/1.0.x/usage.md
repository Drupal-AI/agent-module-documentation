Add floating sticky notes to the site.

---

Floating Sticky Notes adds floating sticky notes — draggable on-screen note widgets (via a block) that stay positioned over the page, useful for reminders, internal annotations, or on-screen prompts. Depends on core `block` and `jquery_ui`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
