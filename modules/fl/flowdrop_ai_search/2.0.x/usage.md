FlowDrop AI Search connects FlowDrop flows to the AI Search vector-database stack.

---

FlowDrop AI Search integrates FlowDrop with the AI Search / vector-database stack, so FlowDrop flows can perform semantic search and retrieval over indexed content — building RAG-style flows within FlowDrop's node/category model.

Embeddings/retrieval run through the AI provider (cost). Depends on `flowdrop`, `flowdrop_node_type`, `flowdrop_node_category`, `ai`, `ai_search`, and `search_api`; requires Drupal 11.
