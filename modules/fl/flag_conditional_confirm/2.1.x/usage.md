Flag Conditional Confirm adds a Conditional Confirm Form link type for Flags.

---

Flag Conditional Confirm adds a **"Conditional Confirm Form" link type** to the Flag module — a flag link
that shows a confirmation form only under configured conditions (rather than always or never), giving finer
control over when flagging/unflagging prompts the user to confirm. It depends on the Flag module, in the Flags
package.

Use it where a flag should conditionally require confirmation. It is a user-engagement/flag feature; flagging
access follows the Flag module's own configuration and it has no access-control role of its own. Choose the
conditional-confirm link type on a flag.
