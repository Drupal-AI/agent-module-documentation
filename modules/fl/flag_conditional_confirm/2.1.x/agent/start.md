Adds a **"Conditional Confirm Form" link type to the Flag module** (show a confirm form only under configured
conditions). Depends on `flag`. Version **2.1.2**. Core `^8||^9||^10||^11`.

User-engagement/flag — flagging access follows the Flag module; no access role of its own.
