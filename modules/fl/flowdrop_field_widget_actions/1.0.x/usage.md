FlowDrop Field Widget Actions integrates Field Widget Actions with FlowDrop workflows to process field content.

---

FlowDrop Field Widget Actions **integrates Field Widget Actions with FlowDrop** — adding buttons/actions to
field widgets that trigger FlowDrop workflows to process the field's content dynamically (e.g. AI/automation on a
field). It depends on the Field Widget Actions module and several FlowDrop modules, and provides its own
permissions.

Use it to run FlowDrop workflows from field widgets. It is a content-editing/automation add-on. Note: the actions
invoke **FlowDrop workflows** which may process content (and, depending on the workflow, call external services) —
review what the triggered workflows do with field content and any egress they perform. It has no access-control
role beyond its permission. Configure the widget actions and workflows.
