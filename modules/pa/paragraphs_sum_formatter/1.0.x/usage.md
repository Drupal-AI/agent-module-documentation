Paragraphs Sum Formatter displays the sum of a configurable field across multi-valued paragraphs.

---

Paragraphs Sum Formatter **displays the sum of a field across paragraphs** — a formatter that totals a
configurable numeric field over all items of a multi-valued paragraph reference (e.g. line-item totals). It depends
on the Paragraphs module.

Use it to show totals of paragraph fields. It is a content-display/field-formatter feature; it computes a sum for
display and has no access-control role. Configure the sum formatter.
