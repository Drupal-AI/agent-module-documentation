Paragraph Anchors adds anchor IDs to paragraphs so they can be deep-linked.

---

Paragraph Anchors automatically generates URL-safe anchor IDs for paragraphs based on their title field — so individual paragraphs (sections) on a page can be deep-linked/anchored, and editors can copy anchor links to specific sections.

Permissions cover settings (`administer paragraph anchors settings`) and copy links (`access paragraph anchor copy links`). Depends on `paragraphs`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
