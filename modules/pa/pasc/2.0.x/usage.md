An interactive checklist of Drupal performance/scalability tasks.

---

Performance and Scalability Checklist (pasc) is an interactive checklist of performance and scalability optimization tasks for Drupal — a guided, checkable list (via ChecklistAPI) of recommended tuning steps so a team can track which optimizations are done. Depends on `checklistapi`; supports Drupal 9.3+.
