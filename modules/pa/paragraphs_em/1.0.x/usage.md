Paragraphs Extended Modal provides a more user-friendly Paragraphs add-modal experience.

---

Paragraphs Extended Modal **improves the Paragraphs add modal** — giving a more user-friendly experience for
choosing and adding paragraph types (better layout/search in the add dialog). It depends on the Paragraphs module
and provides its own permissions.

Use it for a nicer Paragraphs add experience. It is a content-editing/UX feature; it affects the add-paragraph
dialog and has no access-control role beyond its permission. Enable it for the extended modal.
