Validate a paragraph field against another field's value.

---

Paragraph Field Validator validates that when a key field contains an expected text, the value of another field matches a pattern — a conditional validation for Paragraphs so, e.g., when a 'type' field is 'email' the 'value' field must match an email pattern, enforcing consistent paragraph data. Depends on `paragraphs`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
