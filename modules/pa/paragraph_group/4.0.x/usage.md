Paragraph Group adds bulk Field Group functionality and a Details/Accordion based editing widget to the Paragraphs module.

---

Paragraph Group extends the Paragraphs module — adding bulk Field Group functionality and a Details/
Accordion-based editing widget, so paragraph fields can be grouped and edited within collapsible accordion/
details sections, improving the editing UX for paragraph-heavy content. It depends on Paragraphs and is
configured at `paragraph_group.form`.

Use it to organize paragraph editing into grouped accordion sections. It is a content-editing/site-building
feature affecting the paragraphs edit UI; it does not change content or access. Configure the field groups
and editing widget on the paragraph types.
