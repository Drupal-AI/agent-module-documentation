Paragraphs EE Auto Open opens the paragraphs_ee type picker automatically for empty fields.

---

Paragraphs EE Auto Open automatically opens the Paragraphs EE (Editor Experience) type picker on empty paragraphs fields when opted in — so editors immediately see the paragraph-type selection dialog instead of clicking to add, streamlining content creation on pages built with Paragraphs EE.

It's an editorial-UX enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `paragraphs` and `paragraphs_ee`; requires Drupal 11.4+.
