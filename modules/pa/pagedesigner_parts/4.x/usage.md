Pagedesigner Parts is an extension for Pagedesigner enabling reusable page parts.

---

Reusing a designed section across pages — a standard header block, a call-to-action — saves rebuilding it. Pagedesigner Parts enables reusable page parts in Pagedesigner. It extends the page builder; the parts render authored content within the same access model as Pagedesigner. Confirm reusable parts do not embed anything sensitive that would then appear wherever the part is reused.
