Paragraph locator is used to track paragraph usage.

---

Paragraph locator tracks paragraph usage — helping site maintainers find where paragraphs (and paragraph
types) are used across content, useful for auditing, refactoring or cleaning up paragraph-based content. It
provides its own permissions, in the Paragraphs package.

Use it to audit paragraph usage. It is an administration/content-audit feature that reports where paragraphs
are used; it is informational and has no access-control role beyond its permission (gating who can see the
usage report). View the paragraph usage.
