PanKM Chart provides charting/visualization for the PanKM knowledge-management context.

---

PanKM Chart is a custom module providing chart/visualization functionality for the PanKM context — rendering data as charts within Drupal for that project's knowledge-management/reporting needs.

It's a display/visualization module with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
