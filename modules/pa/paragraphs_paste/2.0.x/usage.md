Paragraphs Paste creates paragraphs from pasted content.

---

Paragraphs Paste creates paragraphs from pasted content — when an editor pastes text/media (e.g. a block
of text, an image URL, a video link) into a paragraphs field, it automatically creates the appropriate
paragraph types, speeding up building paragraph-based content. It depends on the Paragraphs module, in the
Paragraphs package.

Use it to speed up paragraph creation from pasted content. It is a content-editing/site-building feature that
parses pasted input into paragraphs; the pasted content becomes authored content (rendered through Drupal's
normal filtering) and it has no access-control role. Configure how pasted content maps to paragraph types.
