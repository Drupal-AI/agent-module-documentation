Page Deletion Guard provides functionality to lock certain pages from deletion.

---

Page Deletion Guard lets administrators lock specific pages (nodes) so they cannot be deleted —
protecting critical pages (home page, legal pages, landing pages) from accidental or unauthorized deletion.
It depends on core Node, provides its own permissions, in the Custom package.

Use it to protect important pages from deletion. It is a content-protection feature: it prevents deletion of
locked nodes (a guardrail against mistakes). Note the scope — it guards **deletion** of the marked pages; it
is a safety/lock feature, and who can lock/unlock pages should be gated by its permission (an admin able to
unlock could still delete). It complements, and does not replace, proper delete permissions. Configure which
pages are locked.
