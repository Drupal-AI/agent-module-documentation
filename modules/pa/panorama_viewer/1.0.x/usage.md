360 panorama viewer creates a field formatter that lets users experience a 360° photo.

---

360 panorama viewer provides an image-field formatter that renders images as interactive 360° panoramas
— letting visitors pan/look around a 360° photo (real-estate, tourism, product views). It depends on core
Image, in the Fields package.

Use it to display 360° photos interactively. It is a content-display/formatter feature; the image is
rendered (as a panorama) respecting normal file access, and it has no access-control role. Select the
panorama formatter on the image field.
