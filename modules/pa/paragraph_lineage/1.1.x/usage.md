Paragraph Lineage allows admins to follow the lineage of a paragraph entity.

---

Paragraph Lineage lets administrators **trace the lineage of a paragraph** — following which host entity
and parent a paragraph belongs to (paragraphs are nested/embedded, so it's otherwise hard to see where one
lives). It depends on the Paragraphs and Views modules, in the Paragraphs package.

Use it to debug/administer where paragraphs are used. It is an administration/developer feature that surfaces
paragraph relationships; it respects the host entities' access and has no access-control role. Use it to view
a paragraph's lineage.
