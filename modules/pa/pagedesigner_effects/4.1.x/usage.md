Pagedesigner Effects is an extension providing visual effects for the Pagedesigner page builder.

---

Pagedesigner Effects extends the Pagedesigner page builder with visual effects — letting editors apply
effects (animations/transitions/visual treatments) to elements built in Pagedesigner. It depends on the
Pagedesigner module, provides its own permissions, in the pagedesigner package.

Use it to add effects to Pagedesigner content. It is a content-display/site-building feature affecting how
Pagedesigner elements are presented; it does not change content or access and has no access-control role.
Configure the effects on Pagedesigner elements.
