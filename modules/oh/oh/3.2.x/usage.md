OH provides an alternative opening hours module.

---

OH (Opening Hours) is an **alternative opening-hours module** — defining business opening times (with
recurring rules via Date Recur), displaying them, and answering "open now?"-style queries, with submodules for
a field (`date_recur_oh_field`), regular hours (`oh_regular`) and reviews (`oh_review`). It depends on the Date
Recur module.

Use it to manage and display opening hours. It is a content-display feature; opening hours are admin/editor
content and it has no access-control role. Configure the opening-hours field and displays.
