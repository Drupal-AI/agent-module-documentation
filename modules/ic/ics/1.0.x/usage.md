A before/after image-comparison slider formatter for image fields.

---

Image Comparison Slider Formatter (ics) adds an Image Comparison Slider formatter to image fields — rendering a two-image field as an interactive before/after slider the visitor drags to compare the two images, useful for renovations, edits, and comparisons. Depends on core `image`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
