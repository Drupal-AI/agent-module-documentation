Usersnap integrates the Usersnap feedback widget with Drupal.

---

Usersnap integrates the **Usersnap** visual-feedback widget — a tool that lets users/testers submit
screenshots and bug reports from the page — by injecting the Usersnap widget with your project's API key. It
provides its own permissions.

Use it to collect visual feedback/bug reports. It is a third-party integration/user-engagement feature — it
loads **Usersnap's third-party JavaScript** (trust the vendor; runs in your origin), feedback (including
screenshots that may capture page content) is sent to **Usersnap** (a data/privacy consideration — be careful
on pages with sensitive data), and the Usersnap **API key** should be handled as config/secret. Its permission
gates who sees the widget. Configure the Usersnap API key.
