Autocomplete username suggestions in the username field by rules.

---

Username Suggestions provides autocomplete suggestions in the username field based on configured rules — so when registering or editing an account, the username field offers rule-based suggested usernames, helping users pick consistent, available names. Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
