Suggest usernames from an email address during registration.

---

Username Suggester suggests usernames based on email using HTMX and Tokens — as a user types their email on the registration form, it proposes available username options (token-derived), speeding up sign-up and reducing username collisions. Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 11.3.
