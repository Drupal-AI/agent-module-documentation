UserWay provides seamless digital accessibility with UserWay.

---

UserWay adds the **UserWay accessibility widget** — a third-party overlay that offers on-page
accessibility adjustments (contrast, text size, spacing, etc.) — by injecting UserWay's script with your
account/site ID. It is in the Accessibility package, core 8+.

Use it to add the UserWay overlay. It is a third-party accessibility/integration feature. Notes to set
expectations: it loads **third-party JavaScript** from UserWay into your pages (trust the vendor; it runs in
your origin) and the widget may process visitor data (a privacy/consent consideration); and an accessibility
**overlay supplements but does not replace** real accessibility work — genuine WCAG conformance comes from the
site's own markup/content, not an overlay. It has no access-control role. Configure the UserWay account/site
ID.
