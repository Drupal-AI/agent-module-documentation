User Registration Reminder sends reminder emails to users.

---

User Registration Reminder **sends reminder emails to users who registered but haven't completed a step** —
e.g. nudging users who never verified their email or logged in, to improve activation. It depends on core User,
provides its own permissions, in the User package.

Use it to re-engage incomplete registrations. It is a user/engagement feature. Data-handling note: it emails
**users** (personal data) automatically on a schedule — respect email/communication preferences and unsubscribe
expectations, and keep the reminder cadence reasonable (avoid nagging). It has no access-control role beyond its
permission. Configure the reminder timing and template.
