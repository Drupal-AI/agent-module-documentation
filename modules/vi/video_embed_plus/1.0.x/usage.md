Auto-add a video embed field with thumbnails to content types.

---

Video Embed Plus generates a video thumbnail token and provides a configurable interface to automatically add a video embed field (`field_video_embed`) to selected content types — supporting YouTube and Vimeo — so builders can bulk-enable video embedding (with thumbnail tokens for teasers) without manually adding the field everywhere. Depends on `token` and `video_embed_field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
