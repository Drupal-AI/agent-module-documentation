Views insert blocks lets you place blocks inside a View's results at specific positions, configured under the View's advanced settings.

---

Views insert blocks allows placing blocks within a View's rendered results at chosen positions —
for example inserting a promo, ad or call-to-action block after the third row of a listing. The option
appears under the View's advanced section, so site builders can interleave block content with View
rows without custom theming. It depends on core Views.

Use it to break up long listings with contextual blocks (ads, related content, CTAs) at predictable
positions. It is a display/site-building feature; the inserted blocks render with their own access and
caching, and this module controls only their placement within the View. No access-control role of its
own.
