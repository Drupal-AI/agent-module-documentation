Vimeo Player integrates with the Vimeo Player SDK.

---

Vimeo Player **integrates the Vimeo Player SDK** — embedding Vimeo videos with programmatic control
(play/pause/events) via Vimeo's JavaScript Player API, for richer video interactions than a plain embed.

Use it to embed controllable Vimeo videos. It is a media/integration feature; the player and video load from
**Vimeo** (third-party embed — the usual privacy note applies), and it has no content or access role. Configure
the Vimeo player on video content.
