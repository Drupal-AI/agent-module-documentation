Views Mailmerge Field adds a merge-style field to build templated text from view rows.

---

Views Mailmerge Field adds a global mail-merge-style view field handler — a field that produces mail-merge/templated output from a view's rows, letting site builders combine row values into a templated string (e.g. for generating letters, labels, or bulk-message text) directly in a view.

It's a Views field-handler utility with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `views`; supports Drupal 8 through 11.
