Video Embed Ted provides a TED handler for Video Embed Fields, letting editors embed TED talks.

---

Video Embed Ted adds a TED provider/handler to the Video Embed Field module — so editors can embed TED
talk videos using the video-embed-field workflow, alongside other providers. It depends on the Video Embed
Field module.

Use it to embed TED talks. It is a media/embed feature; embedded videos are remote content from TED (an
iframe/oEmbed embed), and it has no access-control role. Add the TED provider and embed TED talk URLs.
