Video Embed Peertube provides a Video Embed Field integration with PeerTube, letting editors embed PeerTube videos.

---

Video Embed Peertube adds a PeerTube provider to the Video Embed Field module — so editors can embed
PeerTube videos (the decentralized, self-hostable video platform) using the video-embed-field workflow,
alongside YouTube/Vimeo. It depends on the Video Embed Field module and is configured at
`video_embed_peertube.settings`.

Use it to embed PeerTube videos. It is a media/embed feature; embedded videos are remote content from the
PeerTube instance (an iframe/oEmbed embed), and it has no access-control role. Configure the PeerTube
provider settings.
