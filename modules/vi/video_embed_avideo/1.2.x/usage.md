Video Embed AVideo adds a Video Embed Field handler for self-hosted AVideo.

---

Video Embed AVideo **adds an AVideo handler to Video Embed Field** — letting editors embed videos hosted on a
self-hosted AVideo platform via the Video Embed Field module. It depends on the Video Embed Field module.

Use it to embed AVideo-hosted videos. It is a media/display feature; it embeds video from the configured AVideo
instance and has no access-control role. Note: embeds load content from the AVideo server. Configure the AVideo
embed handler.
