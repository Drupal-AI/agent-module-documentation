An ImageKit video provider for the Video style module.

---

Video style: ImageKit provides an ImageKit video provider for the Video style module — so video-style renditions/delivery URLs are generated through ImageKit's video CDN/transformation service, offloading video processing and delivery.

The ImageKit credentials are admin-configured; store them securely (env-backed). Depends on `video_style`; supports Drupal 11.3+ and 12.
