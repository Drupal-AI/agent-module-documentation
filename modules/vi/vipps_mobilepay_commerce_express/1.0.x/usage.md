Express checkout with Vipps MobilePay, skipping Drupal's checkout.

---

Commerce Vipps MobilePay Express allows customers to quickly check out with Vipps MobilePay, skipping Drupal's own checkout — so a shopper can pay directly via the Vipps/MobilePay express flow (address and details returned by Vipps) without going through the multi-step Commerce checkout, reducing friction.

Payment confirmation runs through the underlying Vipps MobilePay Commerce gateway (official Vipps SDK, server-side status). Depends on `vipps_mobilepay_commerce`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
