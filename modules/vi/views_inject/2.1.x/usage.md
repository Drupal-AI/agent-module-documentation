Views Inject Results lets you inject items from one views display into another, mixing results from multiple views.

---

Views Inject Results lets you inject items from one Views display into another — mixing results from a
secondary view into a primary view's output (for example, interleaving promoted/sponsored items, or
combining two content sources into one listing). It depends on core Views.

Use it to build listings that combine results from multiple views (e.g. inserting featured items among
regular results). It is a content-display/Views feature; the injected items come from their own view and
respect that view's access, so it surfaces content the viewer is entitled to and has no access-control
role of its own. Configure the injecting view and positions.
