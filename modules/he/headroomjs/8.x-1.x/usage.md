Headroom.js integrates the Headroom.js widget, which reacts to scrolling to hide an element (e.g. a menu) on scroll down and show it on scroll up.

---

Headroom.js integrates the Headroom.js JavaScript library, which reacts to a user's scroll direction
to hide an element (typically a sticky header/menu) when scrolling down and reveal it when scrolling
up — freeing screen space while keeping navigation quickly accessible. It is configured at
`headroomjs.settings` (which selector/element to apply it to and behaviour options).

Use it to make a fixed header unobtrusive on long pages. It is a front-end/theming behaviour applied to
a chosen element; it has no content or access implications. Point it at your header/menu selector and
tune the scroll behaviour.
