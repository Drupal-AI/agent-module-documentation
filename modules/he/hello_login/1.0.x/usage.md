Register/log in via the Hellō identity provider (social/email/phone, privacy-first).

---

Hello Login enables users to register and log in using the Hellō Identity Provider — offering their choice of popular social logins, email, or phone, without tracking them — bringing privacy-first OpenID Connect login to Drupal via the Hellō (hello.dev) IdP.

It uses the standard external-auth pattern (`externalauth`); store the Hellō client credentials securely (env-backed). Depends on core `file` and `externalauth`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
