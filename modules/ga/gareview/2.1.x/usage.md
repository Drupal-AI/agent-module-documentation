Google Site Review displays live Google reviews (from Google Business) on a Drupal site.

---

Google Site Review displays live Google reviews — pulling a business's Google reviews (ratings and
comments) and showing them on the Drupal site, useful for social proof on marketing/business sites. It
is in the Google package.

Use it to surface Google Business reviews on the site. It connects to Google's reviews data (via an API/
Place ID and typically an API key), so store any API credentials as secrets and note the review content
comes from Google (availability depends on the external service). It is a content-display/integration
feature showing external review data; the reviews are third-party content, so it has no access-control
role. Configure the business/place and display options.
