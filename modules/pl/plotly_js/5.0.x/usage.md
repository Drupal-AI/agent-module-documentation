Plotly.js integrates the plotly.js charting library with Drupal.

---

Plotly.js integrates the Plotly.js charting library into Drupal — for rendering interactive charts/
graphs (scientific/statistical visualizations) from data, providing a field type and Drush support. It is
configured under `plotly_js`, provides Drush commands, in the Field types package.

Use it to render Plotly.js charts. It is a content-display/visualization feature; the chart data comes from
content/config (respecting its access) and it has no access-control role. Configure the Plotly.js charts.
