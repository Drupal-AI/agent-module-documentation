Plays Games Widget embeds a swipeable feed of HTML5 games on the site.

---

Plays Games Widget adds a configurable, swipeable feed of playable HTML5 games to a Drupal site — so publishers can embed a casual-games feed (from the Plays.org / games provider) to boost engagement and time-on-site.

It embeds third-party game content (privacy/consideration). It's a front-end engagement widget with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
