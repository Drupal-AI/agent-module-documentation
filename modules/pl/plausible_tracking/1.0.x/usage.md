Plausible tracking provides a generalized Plausible Analytics integration for the site.

---

Plausible tracking integrates Plausible Analytics — a privacy-friendly, cookieless web-analytics
service — adding the Plausible tracking script so page views and events are measured without cookies or
collecting personal data. It is configured at `plausible_tracking.settings` and provides its own permissions,
in the Custom package.

Use it for lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics. Notably, Plausible is designed to be **cookieless and
GDPR-friendly** (it doesn't use cookies or collect PII), which reduces the consent burden compared with
cookie-based analytics — though you should still confirm your compliance posture and disclose analytics use
in your privacy policy. It loads a third-party script (Plausible's). It has no access-control role. Configure
the Plausible domain/endpoint.
