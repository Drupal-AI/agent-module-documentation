Field Show All limits a multi-value field to N items with expand/collapse links.

---

Field Show All provides a field formatter configuration that displays only the first N items of a multi-valued field, with 'show all' / 'show less' links to expand or collapse the rest — keeping long multi-value fields compact by default. It's based on Field Load More.

It's a display formatter with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
