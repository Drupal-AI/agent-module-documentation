Field Long Text Pager adds paging to long text fields.

---

Field Long Text Pager **paginates long text fields** — splitting a long text field's content into pages with a
pager on display, for readable long-form content. It provides its own permissions.

Use it to page long content. It is a content-display feature; it paginates rendered text and has no access-control
role beyond its permission. Configure the pager on a text field.
