Field Addons provides additional field-related features and widgets for Drupal fields.

---

Field Addons is a collection of additional field features — extra widgets, formatters or field
behaviours that supplement Drupal's core field tooling. It ships a `field_addons_select2` submodule
(a Select2-based select widget for nicer, searchable dropdowns), indicating its focus on improving
field input/display ergonomics.

Use it to enhance field editing and presentation beyond the core options. It is a
fields/content-editing feature; the additions affect how fields are entered and shown, not access.
Enable the specific sub-features (like the Select2 widget) you want and configure them on the relevant
fields' form/display settings.
