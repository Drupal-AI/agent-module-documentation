Salesforce ECA lets ECA models react to and drive Salesforce push/pull events.

---

Salesforce ECA integrates the Salesforce Suite with ECA (Event-Condition-Action) — providing ECA events, conditions and actions for Salesforce push/pull operations, so no-code ECA models can react to Salesforce sync events or trigger Salesforce operations without custom code.

Salesforce credentials are managed by the Salesforce Suite (store securely/env-backed). Depends on the `salesforce` suite (`salesforce`, `salesforce_mapping`, `salesforce_push`, `salesforce_pull`) and `eca`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
