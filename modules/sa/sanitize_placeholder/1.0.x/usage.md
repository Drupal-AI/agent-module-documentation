Enforce short placeholders and generate fake data on sql:sanitize.

---

Sanitize Placeholder enforces short placeholders and generates fake data after `drush sql:sanitize` — so when a production database is sanitized for a dev/staging copy, fields are replaced with realistic fake values (rather than long placeholder strings), keeping sanitized dumps usable while removing real personal data. It's a developer/privacy tool. Supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
