Sa11y applies the Sa11y accessibility checker.

---

Sa11y applies the Sa11y accessibility checker — an in-page, editor-facing accessibility auditing tool
that flags accessibility issues (missing alt text, heading order, contrast, etc.) directly on the rendered
page as editors review content. It provides its own permissions.

Use it to give editors in-page accessibility feedback. It is an accessibility/editorial feature; it shows an
overlay of accessibility issues (typically to logged-in editors), and it has no access-control role beyond
its permission (gating who sees the checker). Configure where the checker runs.
