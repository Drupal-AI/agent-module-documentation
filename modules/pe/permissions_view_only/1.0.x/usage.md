View the permissions-per-role list without being able to modify it.

---

Permissions List (View Only) provides a way to view the list of permissions per role without being able to modify — a read-only permissions matrix (built on Filter Perms) so auditors/stakeholders can review who has what, without the risk of accidentally changing grants on the editable permissions page. Depends on `filter_perms`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
