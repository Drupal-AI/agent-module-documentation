Send Emails lets you programmatically send emails that can be managed.

---

Send Emails lets you **programmatically send and manage emails** — a manageable email-sending layer (queue/
log/manage sent mail) with `send_emails_attachments` and `send_emails_manual` submodules for attachments and
manual sending. It provides its own permissions, in the Other package.

Use it to send and track outbound email. It is a mail/operations feature. Security note: a system that **sends
email** is powerful — gate its permissions (especially manual sending) to trusted operators to avoid it becoming
a spam/relay vector, keep recipient/content sources trusted, and treat any recipient lists as personal data. It
has no access-control role beyond its permissions. Configure the email sending and management.
