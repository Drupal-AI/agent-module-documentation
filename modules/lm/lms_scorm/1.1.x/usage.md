Load and play SCORM packages within the Drupal LMS.

---

LMS SCORM implements the SCORM feature in Drupal LMS — allowing SCORM packages to be loaded and played within Drupal LMS trainings, with a SCORM player and progress/score tracking committed back to the LMS.

The SCORM player runs client-side; tracking is committed via a public endpoint bound to the learner's session (SCORM's inherent client-side trust model means scores are ultimately client-reported). Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
