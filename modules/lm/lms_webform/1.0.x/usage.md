LMS Webform provides Webform integration for the LMS module.

---

LMS Webform integrates **Webform** with the **LMS** (learning management) module — letting a webform be
used as a course/lesson activity (e.g. a quiz, survey or assignment step) within an LMS course, tying webform
submissions into the learner's progress. It depends on the Webform and LMS modules, in the LMS package.

Use it on LMS sites to add webform-based activities. It is an integration/e-learning feature; webform access
and submission handling follow Webform, and LMS course/enrolment access follows LMS — it adds no access-control
role of its own. Add a webform activity to a course.
