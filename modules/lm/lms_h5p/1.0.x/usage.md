LMS H5P Activity adds an H5P activity plugin for the LMS module.

---

LMS H5P Activity adds an H5P activity plugin to the LMS module — letting course authors include H5P
interactive content (quizzes, interactive video, presentations) as LMS course activities, with results
feeding the LMS. It depends on the LMS module, in the LMS package.

Use it to add H5P activities to LMS courses. It is an e-learning feature; the H5P content is authored course
content, and access follows the LMS course/activity access model. It has no access-control role of its own.
Configure the H5P activity in courses.
