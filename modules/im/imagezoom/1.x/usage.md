Image Zoom provides a field formatter that zooms an image on hover, magnifying the area under the cursor, with an optional gallery submodule.

---

Product and detail images benefit from a hover-zoom — the magnifier-lens effect where moving the cursor over an image shows a magnified view of that area, the standard e-commerce product-image interaction. Image Zoom provides that as a field formatter: set it on an image field and the rendered image magnifies on hover. A `imagezoom_gallery` submodule extends it to galleries of images.

It is a display enhancement with no security surface. As with any JS-library formatter, confirm the library is served appropriately and the effect works with your image markup and sizes — hover-zoom needs a higher-resolution source to magnify into, so the field should reference an image large enough that the zoom shows real detail rather than upscaled blur.

For product catalogues and detail images it is the familiar magnifier interaction. Confirm the source images have enough resolution for the zoom to be useful.
