A gallery variant of the Image Zoom formatter, applying the hover-magnifier effect across a set of images.

---

Extends **imagezoom** to galleries: the hover-zoom magnifier applies to a collection of images rather than a single one, for product-image galleries and multi-image detail views. Governed by the parent's formatter; a display enhancement with no security surface.
