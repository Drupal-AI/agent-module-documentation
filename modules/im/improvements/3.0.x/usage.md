Drupal Improvements bundles assorted small enhancements to Drupal's UX and DX.

---

Drupal Improvements provides a collection of small improvements to Drupal — assorted usability and developer-experience tweaks bundled into one module (built on the Druhels helper library). It's a grab-bag of refinements rather than a single feature.

It's a general enhancement module with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `druhels`; supports Drupal 10 through 15.
