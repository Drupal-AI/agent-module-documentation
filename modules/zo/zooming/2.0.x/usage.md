Zooming integrates a JavaScript image-zoom library so images (and image/media field renders) can be clicked to zoom smoothly.

---

Detail images — a product photo, an artwork, a diagram, a map — benefit from letting the visitor zoom in without navigating to a separate lightbox. Zooming integrates a JS zoom library that expands an image smoothly in place on click, giving that interaction with configuration rather than custom JavaScript.

It applies to image and media renders and is a front-end enhancement with no security surface of its own. As with any JS-library integration, the operational notes are to confirm the library is served locally if third-party origins are a concern, and that the zoom behavior works with your theme's image markup.

For product galleries, portfolios, and any image-detail context, it is a lightweight zoom without a full lightbox. Confirm it targets the right images and does not conflict with a lightbox module if one is also present.
