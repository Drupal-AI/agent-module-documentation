Integrate the NAVU.co sidebar into your website.

---

NAVU integrates the NAVU.co sidebar in your website — embedding NAVU's engagement/navigation sidebar widget (via a block) so visitors get NAVU's on-page features.

The NAVU account/embed credentials are admin-configured and should be stored securely (env-backed). Depends on core `block`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
