A block summarising which users liked a content item.

---

OGCB Likes provides a liker summary block showing which users liked a content item — displaying the list/count of users who flagged (liked) a piece of content, part of the Open Government Community Builder (OGCB) toolset, built on the Flag module. Depends on `flag`; supports Drupal 11+.
