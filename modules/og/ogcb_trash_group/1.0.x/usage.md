Cascade Trash operations across Groups and their related entities.

---

OGCB Trash Group integrates the Trash module with the Group module to cascade trash and restore operations between groups, group relationships, and their related entities — so trashing a group also trashes its content/memberships (and restoring brings them back together), keeping group content consistent in the recycle bin. Depends on `trash` and `group`; supports Drupal 11.1+.
