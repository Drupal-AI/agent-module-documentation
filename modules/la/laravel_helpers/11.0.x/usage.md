Use Laravel helper functions and validation in Drupal.

---

Laravel Helpers adds Laravel helpers and validation to Drupal — making Laravel's convenient helper functions (collections, strings, arrays) and its validation component available to Drupal developers who prefer that API, reducing boilerplate. It's a developer library integration. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
