Add the Lawwwing Consent Management Platform (CMP) to the site.

---

Lawwwing adds the Lawwwing CMP integration to your site — loading the Lawwwing cookie-consent/consent-management widget so the site shows a compliant cookie banner and records consent (GDPR/ePrivacy), managed through a Lawwwing account.

Connect a Lawwwing account (store any credentials securely, env-backed); the consent widget/data is handled by Lawwwing. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
