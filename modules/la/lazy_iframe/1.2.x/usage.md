Lazy Iframe adds the loading="lazy" attribute to all iframes.

---

Lazy Iframe adds the `loading="lazy"` attribute to all `<iframe>` elements on the site — so embedded
iframes (videos, maps, third-party embeds) are lazy-loaded by the browser (fetched only as they approach the
viewport), improving initial page load performance. It is in the Performance package.

Use it to defer iframe loading for performance. It is a performance/display feature affecting iframe markup;
it has no content or access role. Enable it to lazy-load iframes.
