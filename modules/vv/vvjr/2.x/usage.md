---

Progressively revealing content on interaction (click to expand, reveal on demand) is a display pattern. VVJR is an accessible reveal-on-interaction display format for Views. It is a front-end display style with no security surface — it reveals content the view already returns, subject to the view's access. Its selling point is accessibility; confirm the reveal is keyboard-operable and announced to assistive technology.
