WordProof Timestamp creates verifiable timestamps of content via WordProof.

---

WordProof Timestamp lets you 'WordProof' your content — creating verifiable, tamper-evident timestamps of content via the WordProof timestamping service (blockchain-backed), establishing provenance and integrity for published content, and emitting related Schema.org structured data.

WordProof API credentials should be stored securely (env-backed); content metadata is sent to WordProof. Depends on `schema_metatag`, `schema_article`, and `schema_web_page`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
