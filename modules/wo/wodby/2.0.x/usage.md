Wodby integrates the Wodby platform SDK.

---

Wodby **integrates the Wodby SDK** — connecting Drupal to the Wodby hosting/DevOps platform for platform
features (environment info, integrations). It provides its own permissions, in the Wodby package.

Use it on Wodby-hosted sites. It is a hosting/platform integration. Security/data handling: platform integrations
use **platform credentials/tokens** (store as secrets — env — never commit) and may expose environment/operational
details; keep those to trusted admins. It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the Wodby
integration.
