Generate a visual graph of workflow states and transitions.

---

Workflows graph is a helper to generate a graph of workflow states — visualising a Drupal content-moderation/Workflows workflow as a state-transition diagram, so site builders and reviewers can see the states and the allowed transitions between them at a glance. Depends on core `workflows`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
