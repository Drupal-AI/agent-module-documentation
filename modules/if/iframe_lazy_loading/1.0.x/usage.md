Iframe Lazy Loading applies the native loading="lazy" attribute to iframes.

---

Iframe Lazy Loading adds native lazy loading (the `loading="lazy"` attribute) to iframes rendered on the site, so embedded content (videos, maps, widgets) loads only when scrolled into view — improving initial page load performance without custom JavaScript.

It's a performance enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
