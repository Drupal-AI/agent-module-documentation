New Fangled Insight Engine integrates with the New Fangled Insight Engine analytics service.

---

New Fangled Insight Engine **integrates with the Insight Engine** — connecting Drupal to the New Fangled
Insight Engine analytics/insights service to send data and surface insights. It works across core 9.4–11.

Use it to integrate Insight Engine analytics. It is an analytics/integration feature. Security/data handling: it
**sends site/usage data to the external Insight Engine** (egress — may include analytics/PII; disclose per privacy
policy) and authenticates with **credentials/API key** (store as secrets — env/Key — over HTTPS). It has no
access-control role. Configure the Insight Engine credentials.
