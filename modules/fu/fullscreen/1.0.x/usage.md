Fullscreen integrates FullscreenX.js to trigger browser fullscreen mode.

---

Fullscreen **integrates the FullscreenX.js library** — a small, promise-based library to put elements/the
page into the browser's fullscreen mode, for immersive display of content. It works across core 8–11.

Use it to add fullscreen toggles. It is a front-end/UI feature; it runs client-side fullscreen and has no content
or access role. Use the fullscreen integration in your theme/markup.
