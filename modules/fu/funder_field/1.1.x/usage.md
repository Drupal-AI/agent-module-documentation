Funder Field provides a field for funder data sourced from the CrossRef Funder Registry.

---

Funder Field provides a field type for storing funder information as found in the CrossRef Funder Registry — the standard registry of research-funding organizations. It suits academic/research sites that need structured, registry-linked funder metadata on content, with an autocomplete against funder IDs.

Administration is gated by `administer funder_field`. It's a field provider with no front-end access role. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
