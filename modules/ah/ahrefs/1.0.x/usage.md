Ahrefs adds the Ahrefs analytics script to your site.

---

Ahrefs adds the **Ahrefs Web Analytics tracking script** to the site — injecting Ahrefs' analytics snippet
so page views/visitors are recorded in Ahrefs Analytics. It provides its own permissions.

Use it to track traffic with Ahrefs. It is an SEO/analytics feature. Privacy/data handling: it loads a
**third-party tracking script** that collects visitor analytics and sends data to **Ahrefs** — disclose it in
your privacy policy and integrate with your consent tooling where required (analytics scripts are often
consent-gated under GDPR). It has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure the Ahrefs analytics
key.
