Media Entity Dream Broker adds a media source for Dream Broker video embeds.

---

Media Entity Dream Broker provides a media source plugin for Dream Broker — a business video platform — so editors can add Dream Broker videos as media entities (via their URL/ID) and embed them through the Media system, consistent with other media types.

It's a media source with no content or access role of its own; videos are hosted on Dream Broker. Depends on core `media`; supports Drupal 10.1+ and 11.
