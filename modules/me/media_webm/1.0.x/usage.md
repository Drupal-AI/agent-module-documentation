Convert uploaded MP4 files to WebM format.

---

Media: Webm converts uploaded MP4 files to WebM format — so video media uploaded as MP4 also get a WebM derivative, giving browsers a smaller/more-compatible alternative for `<video>` playback. It needs a video encoder (e.g. FFmpeg) available. Depends on core `media`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
