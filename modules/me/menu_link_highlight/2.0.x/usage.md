Menu Link Highlight allows highlighting menu links with a simple checkbox, adding emphasis styling to chosen links.

---

Menu Link Highlight lets editors highlight specific menu links — a simple checkbox on the menu-link
form marks a link for emphasis (a "highlighted"/CTA style), so certain links (a signup, a promotion) stand
out in the menu. It depends on Menu Link Content and provides its own permissions.

Use it to emphasize important menu links (call-to-action items). It is a content-display/navigation feature
adding a highlight flag/class to chosen links; it affects styling, not content or access. Check the box on
the menu links you want highlighted and style the highlight class in your theme.
