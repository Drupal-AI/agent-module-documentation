Membership Manager handles membership lifecycle, plans and subscriptions.

---

Membership Manager provides flexible membership and subscription management — handling the membership lifecycle (plans, joining, renewal, expiry) so organizations, clubs, and associations can manage members and their subscription status within Drupal.

Permissions separate plan administration (`administer membership plans`), membership administration (`administer memberships`, `administer membership manager`), and self-service (`view own membership`) — grant the administer permissions to trusted staff. Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
