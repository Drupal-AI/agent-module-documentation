Hide placeholder parent menu items that have no visible children.

---

Menu Hide Parent automatically hides placeholder parent menu items when they have no visible child links after access checks — so a parent menu entry that only exists to group children disappears when all its children are hidden (by permission/publication), keeping the menu clean and avoiding dead parent links. Supports Drupal 10.4+ and 11.
