Melhor Envio adds Brazilian shipping-rate calculation to Commerce via the Melhor Envio API.

---

Melhor Envio Commerce Shipping integrates the Melhor Envio service (a Brazilian shipping aggregator) with Drupal Commerce to calculate shipping rates — so Brazilian stores can offer accurate carrier rates (Correios and others) at checkout, fetched from the Melhor Envio API.

Melhor Envio API credentials should be stored securely (env-backed). Depends on `commerce_shipping`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
