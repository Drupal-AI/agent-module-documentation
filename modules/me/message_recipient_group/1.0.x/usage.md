Collect message recipients from a Group.

---

Message recipient group collects message recipients from a group — so a Message's recipients can be resolved from the membership of a Group (via the Group module), letting notification flows target 'everyone in group X'. Depends on `message_recipient_entity`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
