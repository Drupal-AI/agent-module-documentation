Metatag Search.gov provides specific metatags for Search.gov.

---

Metatag Search.gov **provides Search.gov-specific meta tags** — adding the meta tags that the US
Search.gov federal search service uses to index/classify content, via the Metatag module. It depends on the
Metatag module, in the SEO package.

Use it on US-government sites indexed by Search.gov. It is an SEO feature adding structured metadata; it reflects
on-page content and has no content or access role. Configure the Search.gov meta tags.
