A before/after Juxtapose slider formatter for image fields.

---

Juxtapose Slider Image Formatter adds a Juxtapose before/after slider formatter to image fields — rendering a two-image field as an interactive JuxtaposeJS slider the visitor drags to compare before and after images. Depends on core `image`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
