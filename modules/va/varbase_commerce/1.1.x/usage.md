A Varbase starting point for a merchandise section and product catalog.

---

Varbase Commerce provides a starting point for the Varbase merchandise section and the products catalog — a Varbase (Drupal distribution) component that scaffolds Drupal Commerce products, a catalog, and related config so a Varbase site starts with a working store/merchandise section. It bundles and configures the Commerce stack. Requires Drupal 11.4.
