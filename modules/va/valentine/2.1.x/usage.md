Let site users send valentines to each other.

Valentine (❤️) provides functionality for sending valentines to other site users — a fun/social feature letting members send a valentine greeting to another user on the site, for community engagement around Valentine's Day. Depends on `service` (>=3.0.0); supports Drupal 10.3+.
