---

Varbase AI provides a collection of recipes for AI tools tailored to the Varbase distribution —
empowering editorial teams with AI capabilities (content assistance, summarization, generation, etc.) and
managing custom AI integrations/workflows for Varbase in an updatable way. It is in the Varbase package.

Use it on Varbase sites to enable curated AI tooling. It is an integration/setup feature (recipes) that wires
up AI capabilities. Security/data notes: AI features send content to the configured AI provider(s) — **store
provider API keys as secrets** and be mindful of sending sensitive content to external AI services; the
recipes configure integrations, so review what they enable. It has no access-control role. Apply the recipes
and configure the AI providers.
