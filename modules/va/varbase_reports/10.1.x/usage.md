Varbase Reports adds user and content statistical reports with export.

---

Varbase Reports provides a number of statistical reports — user reports and content reports — for Varbase (and general Drupal) sites, built on Views with better exposed filters, CSV export, and date handling, so site operators get ready-made reporting dashboards.

Permissions cover user reports (`access varbase users reports`) and content reports (`access varbase content reports`); reports can expose user/content data, so restrict to trusted roles. Depends on core `user`/`node`/`views` plus better_exposed_filters, views_data_export, and csv_serialization; requires Drupal 11.4.
