Vertex AI Search Promoted Results manages promoted/promotional items for Vertex AI Search.

---

Vertex AI Search Promoted Results (vais_promos) **manages promoted results for Vertex AI Search** — letting
admins define promotional items that are boosted/pinned in Vertex AI Search result pages. It depends on the Vertex
AI Search module and provides its own permissions.

Use it to promote items in AI search. It is a search/administration feature; promotions are admin-configured and it
has no access-control role beyond its permission. Note: promotion config interacts with the Vertex AI Search
(Google Cloud) integration — provider credentials there should be secrets. Configure the promotions.
