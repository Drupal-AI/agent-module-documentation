A button to invalidate 'everything' via the Purge module.

---

VarnishVanish provides a button that ties into the Purge module's invalidate 'everything' option — giving administrators a one-click way to purge the entire Varnish/reverse-proxy cache (via Purge's everything invalidation), useful after bulk changes. Depends on `purge` and `purge_processor_cron`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
