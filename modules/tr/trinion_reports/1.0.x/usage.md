Reporting pages for the Trinion setup (e.g. payments received).

---

Trinion reports provides reporting pages for Trinion-based sites — permission-gated report views such as 'Payments Received', giving administrators structured reports over the Trinion data model. Reports are behind specific permissions. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
