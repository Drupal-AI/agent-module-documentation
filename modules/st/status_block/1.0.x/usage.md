Status Block displays configurable site-state information in a block.

---

Status Block provides a customizable block that shows site state — configurable status information (e.g. maintenance state, environment, custom indicators) rendered in a block region, useful for surfacing operational status to staff or visitors.

Viewing is gated by `view status blocks`. It's a display/admin utility with no content role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
