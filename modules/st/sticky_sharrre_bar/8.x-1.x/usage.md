A sticky social-share bar using the Sharrre library.

---

Sticky Sharrre Bar provides a sticky Sharrre block on your site — a floating social-share bar (using the Sharrre library) that sticks to the side/edge of the page with share buttons/counts for major social networks, encouraging sharing. Depends on core `block`; supports Drupal 8.8 through 11.
