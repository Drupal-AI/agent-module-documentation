Synfilters extends Better Exposed Filters.

---

Synfilters **extends Better Exposed Filters (BEF)** — adding additional exposed-filter widgets/behaviours
for Views exposed filters, beyond what BEF provides. It depends on the Better Exposed Filters module, in the
SynapseF package.

Use it to enhance Views exposed filters. It is a content-display/Views feature affecting the exposed-filter UI;
results respect the View's access and it has no access-control role. Configure the exposed filters on a View.
