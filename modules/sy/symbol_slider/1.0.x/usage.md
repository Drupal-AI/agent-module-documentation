Symbol Slider creates configurable symbol/icon slider entities.

---

Symbol Slider lets you generate different types of symbol sliders — sliders composed of symbols/icons managed as slider entities, for displaying scrolling sets of symbols (e.g. partner logos, feature icons) on the front end.

It exposes CRUD permissions on slider entities (`add`/`view`/`edit`/`delete slider entity`). Depends on core `options` and `user`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
