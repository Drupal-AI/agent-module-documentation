Synapse Staff provides Synapse (Synatix) Drupal customizations, typically for staff-related content management.

---

Synapse Staff (synapse) provides Synapse/Synatix-specific Drupal customizations — a site-specific module
(from the Synatix/Synapse family) that adds customizations, commonly around staff content management. It is
configured at `synapse.settings`. As a vendor/site-specific customization module, its features are tailored
to that context rather than a general-purpose capability.

Use it where the Synapse/Synatix customizations are needed. It is an administration/customization module;
its behaviour depends on the specific customizations it provides. Because such modules can touch various site
areas, review what it does in your context. It has no documented general access-control role. Configure the
Synapse settings.
