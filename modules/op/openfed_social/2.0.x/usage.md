Openfed Social Links provides links to share content on social networks.

---

Openfed Social Links **provides social-share links** — buttons/links to share the current page on social
networks, part of the Openfed distribution. It works on core 10.3–11.

Use it to add share links. It is a user-engagement feature; the share links point to social networks (loading their
content when clicked) and it has no access-control role. Configure the social-share links.
