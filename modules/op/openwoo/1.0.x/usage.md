A pluggable framework for publishing and searching Woo (Dutch open-government) publications.

---

OpenWoo is a pluggable framework for publishing and searching Woo (Wet open overheid) publications — so a Dutch government site can publish its open-government documents in the standard WOO format and expose/search them, meeting the Dutch open-government transparency obligations.

It publishes public government documents (by design). Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
