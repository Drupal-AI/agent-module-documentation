Open Vocabularies lets you choose how content is categorised without having to change the related fields.

---

Open Vocabularies lets editors **categorise content flexibly** — it decouples the "vocabulary"
(a configurable association to any reference target, not just taxonomy) from the fields, so how a piece of
content is categorised can change without altering the field configuration on each bundle. It provides its own
permissions, in the OpenEuropa package.

Use it for reusable, cross-bundle categorisation associations. It is a site-structure/content-modelling
feature; associations reference other entities (whose access still applies) and it has no access-control role
beyond its permission. Configure the vocabulary associations.
