Allow users to register without an email address.

---

Optional Email allows anonymous users to register without an email — making the email field optional on the user registration form, for sites (e.g. phone-based or internal) where email isn't required.

Security consideration: accounts without an email cannot use email-based password reset, and email is often a key identity/anti-abuse signal — only enable this if your registration flow has an alternative verification and recovery path. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
