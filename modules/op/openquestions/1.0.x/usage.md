A voting module that surfaces the best content, not just the most popular.

---

OpenQuestions is a voting module that finds the best content, not just the most popular — using a voting/ranking model (beyond raw like counts) to surface high-quality questions/content, useful for Q&A and deliberation sites. Depends on core `block`, `taxonomy`, and `views`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
