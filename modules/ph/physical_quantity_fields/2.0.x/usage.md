Physical Quantity Fields adds fields for physical quantities with units and conversion.

---

Physical Quantity Fields provides field types, widgets and formatters for physical quantities — length, mass, temperature, and other measurable quantities — with unit handling, so content can store measurements as structured, unit-aware values (e.g. for product specs, scientific data).

It's a field provider with no content or access role of its own; render output is escaped normally. Depends on core `field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
