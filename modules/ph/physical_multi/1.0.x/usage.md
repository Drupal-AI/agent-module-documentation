A single field storing a weight, volume, count, or size measurement.

---

Physical Multi Field provides a single field that can store a weight, volume, count, or size measurement (for recipe ingredients) — so one field flexibly holds any of those physical quantities with its unit, useful for recipes and product specs. Depends on core `field` and `physical`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
