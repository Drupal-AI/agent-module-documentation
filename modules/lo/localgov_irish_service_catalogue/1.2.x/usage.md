LocalGov Irish Service Catalogue creates service-catalogue listings for Irish councils.

---

LocalGov Irish Service Catalogue **creates service-catalogue listings for Irish councils** — content types,
Views, facets and migrations for publishing a catalogue of council services, part of the LocalGov Drupal
distribution. It depends on Search API, Facets, Migrate Plus/Tools and LocalGov Multilingual Services, and provides
its own permissions.

Use it to build an Irish council service catalogue. It is a distribution/content-model feature; it scaffolds
content types and search and has no access-control role beyond its permission. It expects the LocalGov Drupal
distribution. Configure the service catalogue.
