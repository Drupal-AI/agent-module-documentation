LocalGov Entity Share tailors Entity Share (content syndication) for LocalGov Drupal.

---

LocalGov Entity Share provides Entity Share client functionality specific to LocalGov Drupal — configuring and extending the Entity Share module so LocalGov councils can pull/share content (pages, services) between Drupal sites in the LocalGov ecosystem.

It builds on `entity_share_client`; content is pulled from remote Drupal sites via JSON:API, so configure remote credentials securely and trust the source sites (imported content and its access follow Entity Share's model). Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
