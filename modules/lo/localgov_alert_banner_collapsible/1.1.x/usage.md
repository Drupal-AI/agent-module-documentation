A collapsible variant of the LocalGov alert banner.

---

LocalGov Alert Banner Collapsible provides a collapsible alert banner — extending the LocalGov Alert Banner so the site-wide alert can be collapsed/expanded by the visitor (rather than always fully shown), useful for persistent-but-dismissible notices on LocalGov (UK council) sites. Depends on `localgov_alert_banner`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
