LocalGov EU Cookie Compliance provides a block that embeds the EU Cookie settings form.

---

LocalGov EU Cookie Compliance provides a **block that embeds the EU Cookie Compliance settings form** — so
a LocalGov Drupal site can place the cookie-consent preferences form (from the EU Cookie Compliance module) in
a region/page, letting visitors review and update their cookie choices. It depends on the EU Cookie Compliance
module, in the LocalGov Drupal package.

Use it on LocalGov sites to surface the cookie settings form as a block. It is a privacy/consent presentation
layer; the actual consent handling is done by **EU Cookie Compliance** (this just embeds its form), and it has
no access-control role. Place the cookie-settings block.
