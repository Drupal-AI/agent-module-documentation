LocalGov Alert Banner provides sitewide emergency banners: a dedicated content entity with its own bundles, moderation workflow and per-bundle permissions, rendered by a block that shows the highest-priority published banner wherever it is placed.

---

Banners are `localgov_alert_banner` content entities — not nodes — with a `localgov_alert_banner_type` bundle entity so a site can distinguish, say, routine notices from major incidents. The shipped bundle carries `short_description`, `link`, `type_of_alert` (the severity/priority list) and `visibility`, a `condition_field` value letting editors restrict a banner to particular pages or contexts. `AlertBannerManager::getCurrentAlertBanners()` is the single source of truth for what is live: it queries published banners with `accessCheck(TRUE)`, sorts by `type_of_alert` descending (only if that field storage exists — the module degrades gracefully when a site removes it) then by `changed` descending, optionally filters to selected bundles, loads each with `getTranslationFromContext()` and checks `view` access per banner. Deliberately it loads **all** published banners before applying the visibility check, so cache contexts from every candidate are collected and the block's cacheability is correct. `AlertBannerBlock` renders the result and invalidates on banner changes. Around that core sits a full editorial stack: a `localgov_alert_banners` content moderation workflow, an `emergency_publisher` role, an admin view at `/admin/content/alert-banners`, revision support with a custom storage handler (revision queries, per-user revisions, language clean-up), translation handling, and integration hooks for Scheduled Transitions and the Gin admin theme. Permissions are split between global ones (`access … listing page`, `view all …`, `manage all …` and the restricted `administer … types`) and per-bundle permissions generated by `AlertBannerEntityPermissions`.

- Publish a sitewide emergency banner during a major incident.
- Warn residents about severe weather across every page.
- Show a service-disruption notice on selected pages only.
- Distinguish routine notices from critical alerts by banner type.
- Order competing banners by severity automatically.
- Give an emergency communications team its own publishing role.
- Moderate banners through a workflow before they go live.
- Schedule a banner to publish and unpublish automatically.
- Restrict a banner to a section of the site with visibility conditions.
- Translate banners for multilingual audiences.
- Keep an audit trail of banner revisions.
- Review all banners from a dedicated admin listing.
- Let editors preview a banner before publishing.
- Take a banner down instantly by unpublishing it.
- Show only the most severe active banner site-wide.
- Place the banner block in any region of any theme.
- Give different services their own banner types.
- Grant per-bundle permissions so teams only manage their own banners.
- Ensure banner caching stays correct when visibility varies by page.
- Clean up banner revisions for a removed language.
- Integrate banner editing with the Gin admin theme's content forms.
- Provide a linked call to action inside the banner.
- Keep emergency messaging out of the node system entirely.
- Deploy banner types and workflow as configuration.
