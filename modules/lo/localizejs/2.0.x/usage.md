Localize.js provides integration with localizejs.com.

---

Localize.js integrates the **Localize.js (localizejs.com)** service — a JavaScript-based translation/
localization platform that translates page content on the client — by injecting the Localize.js widget with
your project key. It provides its own permissions, in the Multilingual package.

Use it to add Localize.js client-side translation. It is a third-party integration/multilingual feature — it
loads **Localize.js third-party JavaScript** (trust the vendor; it runs in your origin and reads/translates
page content), page content is sent to/processed by **Localize.js** (a data/privacy consideration), and the
project **key** should be handled as config/secret. It has no access-control role beyond its permission.
Configure the Localize.js project key.
