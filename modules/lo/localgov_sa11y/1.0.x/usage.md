LocalGov Sa11y applies the Sa11y accessibility checker to LocalGov Drupal websites, surfacing accessibility issues to editors.

---

LocalGov Sa11y integrates the Sa11y accessibility checker into LocalGov Drupal sites — Sa11y is a
front-end accessibility auditing tool that highlights accessibility issues (missing alt text, heading
problems, contrast, etc.) directly on the page for content editors, helping them fix issues in context. It
is oriented toward the LocalGov Drupal distribution and provides its own permissions.

Use it on LocalGov sites to give editors in-page accessibility feedback. It is an accessibility/editorial
tooling feature; it surfaces issues to editors (typically only shown to authenticated editors via its
permission) and has no content or access role beyond that gating. Restrict the checker to editors (visitors
shouldn't see the audit overlay). Configure who sees Sa11y.
