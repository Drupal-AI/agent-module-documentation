Login Monitor provides comprehensive login activity tracking with notifications, logging and statistical reports.

---

Login Monitor provides comprehensive login-activity tracking — logging login events, sending
notifications (e.g. on suspicious/new logins), and offering statistical reports of login activity, giving
administrators visibility into who logs in, when and from where. It is configured at `login_monitor.settings`
and provides its own permissions, in the Other package.

Use it for login visibility/auditing and alerting. This is a **positive** security-monitoring feature:
tracking logins helps detect compromised accounts, brute-force attempts and unusual access, and login
notifications alert users/admins to unexpected logins. Keep in mind: login logs record user/IP data
(personal data — handle per privacy, gate the reports with its permissions to trusted admins). It has no
access-control role (it monitors, it doesn't block by itself). Configure the tracking, notifications and
reports.
