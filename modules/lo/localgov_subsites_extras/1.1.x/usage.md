LocalGov Subsites Extras allows creating subsites that use the menu system to determine their page hierarchy, extending the LocalGov Drupal subsites feature.

---

LocalGov Subsites Extras extends the LocalGov Drupal "subsites" concept — self-contained mini-sites
(microsites) within a larger LocalGov site — by letting their page hierarchy be driven by the menu
system. Instead of a fixed subsite structure, editors define the subsite's navigation and page
relationships through menus (depends on core `menu_ui` and `node`), so the menu hierarchy determines
how subsite pages nest and how navigation is generated.

Use it on a LocalGov Drupal site that builds campaign or department microsites and wants their
structure managed via menus. It is a site-structure/editorial feature layered on the LocalGov subsites
system; it defines page hierarchy and navigation, not access control. It fits within the LocalGov
ecosystem and expects the base subsites functionality to be present.
