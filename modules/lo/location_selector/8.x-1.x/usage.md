Location Selector lets you assign content to places.

---

Location Selector **lets editors assign content to one or more locations** — a UI/field to tag content with
places/regions, for location-based organization or filtering. It is in the Location Selector package.

Use it to tag content by location. It is a content-editing/fields feature; the location data is admin/editor
content and it has no access-control role. Assign locations to content.
