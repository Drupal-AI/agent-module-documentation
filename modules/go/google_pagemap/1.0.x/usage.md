Google PageMap Embed adds PageMap structured data to pages for Google indexing.

---

Google PageMap Embed injects Google PageMap data onto pages — structured metadata Google can use for advanced indexing of custom search results (e.g. Programmable Search Engine). It helps expose structured attributes to Google's crawler.

It's an SEO/metadata enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `preprocessors`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
