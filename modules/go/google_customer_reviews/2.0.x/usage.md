Google Customer Reviews integrates Google Customer Reviews with Commerce.

---

Google Customer Reviews **integrates Google Customer Reviews with Drupal Commerce** — adding the
post-purchase opt-in survey badge so customers can be invited (by Google) to review their purchase, feeding
Google's seller ratings. It depends on Commerce and Commerce Checkout, in the Commerce (contrib) package.

Use it to collect Google seller reviews. It is an e-commerce/integration feature. Data-handling note: it passes
**order details (including the customer email and order id) to Google** to trigger the survey (external egress of
PII — disclose per your privacy policy), via Google's snippet. It has no access-control role. Configure the
Google merchant settings.
