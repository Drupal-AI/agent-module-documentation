Bontact embeds the Bontact live-chat/contact widget on the site.

---

Bontact integrates the Bontact service — a live-chat/contact widget platform — by embedding its widget on Drupal pages, so visitors can contact the site via chat, messaging, or other Bontact channels.

The widget loads Bontact's third-party script (review privacy/data-flow). It's a front-end integration with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
