App Banners provides support for Apple Smart App Banners and Android app banners, prompting mobile visitors to open or install a native app.

---

Sites with a companion mobile app want to nudge mobile-web visitors toward it. App Banners adds the Apple Smart App Banner meta tag and the Android equivalent, so mobile browsers show the native banner. It is a marketing/meta-tag feature with no security surface — it emits standard app-association meta tags. Confirm the app IDs are correct, since a wrong ID points visitors at the wrong app.
