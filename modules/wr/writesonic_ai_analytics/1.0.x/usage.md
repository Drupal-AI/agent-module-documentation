Writesonic AI Analytics adds Writesonic AI analytics to improve LLM visibility of your site.

---

Writesonic AI Analytics **adds Writesonic AI analytics** — integrating Writesonic's analytics to track/improve
how visible your site's content is to large language models (LLM/AI search visibility). It is in the Statistics
package.

Use it to monitor AI/LLM visibility. It is an analytics/integration feature. Security/data handling: it integrates
the **external Writesonic service** — typically loading a **third-party analytics script** and/or sending data to
Writesonic (egress; disclose per privacy policy, mind tracking consent), authenticated with an **API key/site ID**
(store as configuration/secret as appropriate) over HTTPS. It has no access-control role. Configure the Writesonic
integration.
