Rijksvideo displays video from the Rijksvideo media source (a Dutch government video platform).

---

Dutch government sites using the Rijksvideo platform can embed its videos. Rijksvideo displays video from that media source. It embeds third-party video, so the consideration is that embedding loads the platform's player (a third-party origin), and any embed may carry the platform's tracking — gate behind consent where required. For a Rijksvideo-using site it embeds the videos.
