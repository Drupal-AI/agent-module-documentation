Integrates the Handlebars.js templating library.

---

Handlebars provides integration with Handlebars JS — making the Handlebars.js client-side templating library available to Drupal (as a library/asset) so themes and JS can render Handlebars templates in the browser. It's a frontend/developer library integration. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
