HAL Publications displays publications from HAL (Hyper Articles en Ligne).

---

HAL Publications **displays publications from HAL** (Hyper Articles en Ligne) — the French open research
archive — pulling a researcher's/lab's publication list from the HAL API and rendering it on the site. It
depends on core User and the Chosen module, provides its own permissions, in the Content package.

Use it to show HAL publications. It is an integration/content feature. Security/data handling: it calls the
**HAL API** over the network (use HTTPS; if any credentials are needed, store as secrets) and the publication
data comes from HAL (external content). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the HAL
query/collection.
