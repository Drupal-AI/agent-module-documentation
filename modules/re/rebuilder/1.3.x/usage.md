Rebuild specific cached items without a full cache clear.

---

Rebuilder lets you rebuild something cached by Drupal without doing a full cache clear — targeting specific caches/derived data to rebuild so operators can refresh just what's stale without the site-wide performance hit of a complete `drush cr`. Supports Drupal 10.4+ and 11.1+.
