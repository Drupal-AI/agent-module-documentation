Show readers the estimated remaining reading time.

---

ReadRemaining tells your readers how long they'll need to get through your content — displaying an estimated remaining reading time indicator on content so visitors know how much is left, making long articles feel more approachable (a TL;DR-style aid). Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
