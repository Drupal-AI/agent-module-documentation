Remove diacritics strips accents/diacritics from strings (e.g. for slugs or search).

---

Remove diacritics removes accents and diacritics from text — converting characters like é/ñ/ü to their base forms — useful for generating clean URL slugs, normalizing search input, or producing ASCII-safe strings from accented content.

It's a text-processing utility with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
