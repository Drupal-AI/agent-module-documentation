Let reviewers add tagged notes anchored to page sections.

---

Reviewer Notes allows reviewers to add tagged notes anchored to any section of a page — so content reviewers/QA can leave contextual annotations pinned to specific parts of a rendered page (like sticky-note review comments), with tags for categorisation, aiding editorial review workflows. Depends on core `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
