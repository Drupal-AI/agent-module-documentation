Report on redirects created by the Redirect module.

---

Redirect Review reviews the redirects created through the Redirect module and generates a report — giving administrators a Views-based overview of existing redirects (source, target, status) so they can audit, find loops/duplicates and clean up the redirect set. Depends on `redirect` and core `views`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
