Detect overlapping date ranges across content of a type.

---

Resource Conflict detects overlapping date ranges for configured content types — validating that a new/edited node's date-range field doesn't overlap existing nodes (e.g. double-booked rooms, equipment or staff), so booking-style content can prevent scheduling conflicts. Depends on core `node` and `datetime_range`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
