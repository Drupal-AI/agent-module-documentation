Require users to re-enter their password on sensitive pages.

---

Reauthenticate requires reauthentication on certain pages — forcing an already-logged-in user to re-enter their password (step-up authentication) before accessing configured sensitive routes, so a walked-away or hijacked session cannot reach high-risk areas without proving the password again. Supports Drupal 9.4+, 10, and 11.
