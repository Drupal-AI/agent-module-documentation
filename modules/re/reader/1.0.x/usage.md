Reader provides a PWA reader.

---

Reader provides a **Progressive Web App (PWA) reader** — a reading-focused, app-like front end for
consuming site content (offline-capable, installable), with a `reader_theme` submodule for its presentation. It
provides its own permissions.

Use it to offer an app-like reading experience. It is a front-end/user-engagement feature; the content it
surfaces follows the site's normal content access, and it has no access-control role beyond its permission.
Configure and enable the reader.
