Preload Google reCAPTCHA v2 Checkbox for faster/cleaner forms.

---

reCAPTCHA Preloader provides a preloader for Google reCAPTCHA v2 Checkbox — deferring/optimising when the reCAPTCHA widget loads so forms with a v2 checkbox render faster and the CAPTCHA appears smoothly, improving form UX. Depends on `captcha` (>=1.9.0) and `recaptcha`; supports Drupal 11+.
