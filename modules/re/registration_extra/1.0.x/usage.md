Registration Extra adds extra functionality to the Registration module.

---

Registration Extra **adds functionality to the Registration module** — extending the Registration (event/entity
registration) module with additional features/behaviors. It depends on the Registration module.

Use it to extend Registration. It is a registration/user-engagement add-on. Security note: because it touches
**registration** (which can involve capacity, approval and per-registration data), verify any behavior it adds
preserves your registration access controls (who may register, approval requirements) and handle registrant data
(PII) per your privacy policy. It has no explicit access-control role. Configure the extra registration features.
