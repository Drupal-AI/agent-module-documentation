Redirect Expire lets redirects expire and be cleaned up automatically.

---

Redirect Expire adds expiration functionality to the Redirect module — redirects can be given an expiry after which they are automatically removed, so temporary redirects (campaigns, short-lived moves) don't accumulate forever and clutter the redirect table.

It's a redirect-management enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `redirect`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
