Revision Extras provides bug fixes and additional options for core content revisions.

---

Revision Extras **adds options and fixes for core revisions** — improving Drupal's content revision handling
with extra options and bug fixes (e.g. around revision display/management). It works across core 10.2–12.

Use it to enhance revision handling. It is a content-editing feature; revision access still follows core revision
permissions and it has no access-control role. Configure the revision options.
