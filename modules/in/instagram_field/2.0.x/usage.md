Instagram Field provides an Instagram recent post field.

---

Instagram Field provides a **field that displays recent Instagram posts** — showing a user's/account's
latest Instagram content on an entity, via Instagram's API/embed. It depends on core Field, provides its own
permissions, in the Field types package.

Use it to show an Instagram feed on content. It is a fields/media/integration feature. Security/data handling:
it fetches from **Instagram** (external — the account/token used is configured; store any **access token** as a
secret over HTTPS), and posts/assets load from Instagram (third-party). It has no access-control role beyond its
permission. Configure the Instagram field and credentials.
