Info Banner displays a configurable banner at page top, in a div by ID, or as a popup, with wildcard path support and Varnish compatibility.

---

Info Banner displays a configurable informational banner — shown at the top of the page, inside a div by
ID, or as a popup — with support for wildcard path targeting (show on specific paths) and Varnish
compatibility (so it works with edge caching). It ships an `info_banner_blocks` submodule, is configured at
`info_banner.settings`, provides its own permissions, in the Utility package.

Use it to show site-wide or path-specific informational banners. It is a content/display feature; the banner
is admin-authored content shown to visitors, and it has no access-control role. Gate who can configure
banners with its permissions. Configure the banner content and paths.
