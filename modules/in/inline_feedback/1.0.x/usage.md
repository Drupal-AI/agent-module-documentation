Leave editorial comments directly in the content.

---

Inline Feedback facilitates editorial collaboration by leaving specific comments directly in the content — so reviewers/editors can attach inline comments to parts of a page (like document review comments) that other editors see, streamlining editorial review without external tools.

Feedback comments are editorial/internal — gate the submit/view permissions so only editorial staff can leave and read them (the submit route is public by design for logged-in reviewers; restrict as needed). Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
