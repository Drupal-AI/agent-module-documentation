An OpenID Connect client for Infomaniak login.

---

Infomaniak OpenID Connect is an OpenID Connect client for Infomaniak — letting users log in to Drupal with their Infomaniak account via the standard OpenID Connect module, so Infomaniak becomes an SSO identity provider for the site.

The Infomaniak client id/secret are admin-configured and should be stored securely (env-backed). Depends on `openid_connect`; supports Drupal 10.1+, 11, and 12.
