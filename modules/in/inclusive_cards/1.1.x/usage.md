Inclusive Cards provides functionality for making accessible cards.

---

Inclusive Cards provides **functionality for building accessible "card" components** — card UI patterns
(image/title/link) implemented with accessibility (keyboard, screen-reader, whole-card clickability done
correctly) in mind. It is in the Custom package.

Use it to add accessible cards to layouts. It is a theming/content-display feature; card content is admin/editor
-authored and it has no content or access role. Configure and place the cards.
