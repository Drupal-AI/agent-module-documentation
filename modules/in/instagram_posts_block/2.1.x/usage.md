Instagram Posts Block provides a block showing an Instagram feed.

---

Instagram Posts Block **provides a block showing an Instagram feed** — displaying recent posts from an
Instagram account in a block on the site. It works on core 11.

Use it to embed an Instagram feed. It is a social-media/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **fetches
posts from Instagram/Meta** (external egress) and typically needs an **access token/credentials** for the Instagram
API — store those as secrets (env/Key) over HTTPS, and be mindful that embedding third-party content loads
Instagram/Meta assets (privacy/consent considerations). It has no access-control role. Configure the Instagram
credentials.
