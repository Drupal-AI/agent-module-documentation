YouTube No-Cookie provides a youtube-nocookie handler for Video Embed Field.

---

YouTube No-Cookie provides a **youtube-nocookie handler for Video Embed Field** — embedding YouTube videos
via YouTube's privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain, which defers YouTube's tracking cookies until the
viewer plays the video. It depends on the Video Embed Field module.

Use it for more privacy-friendly YouTube embeds. It is a media/privacy feature; the video still loads from
**YouTube** (privacy-enhanced domain — fewer cookies until play, but not zero third-party contact), and it has
no content or access role. Use the nocookie handler on video fields.
