Facilitates and enhances access to entity field values.

---

Glint facilitates and enhances access of field values for entities — a developer helper/API that makes reading field values off entities simpler and more robust (handling multi-value, references, defaults), reducing boilerplate in code and templates. It's a developer/entity utility. Supports Drupal 8.8 through 11.
