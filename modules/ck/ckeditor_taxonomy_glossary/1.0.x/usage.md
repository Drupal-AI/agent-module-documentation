CKEditor Taxonomy Glossary links text to taxonomy glossary terms and shows tooltips in CKEditor 5.

---

CKEditor Taxonomy Glossary lets editors link words in rich text to taxonomy-based glossary terms, showing the term definition as a tooltip on the front end. It combines a CKEditor 5 plugin, a text filter, and a taxonomy vocabulary to build an interactive glossary.

Permissions cover administration (`administer glossary terms`), linking (`link to glossary terms`), and in-editor term creation (`create glossary terms via editor`) — restrict term creation to trusted editors. Depends on core `taxonomy`, `ckeditor5`, and `filter`; supports Drupal 10.1+, 11, and 12.
