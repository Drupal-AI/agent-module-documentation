CKEditor Citation integrates the CKEditor 5 citations plugin.

---

CKEditor Citation integrates a **CKEditor 5 citations plugin** — letting editors add citations/references
to rich-text content (mark quoted or sourced text with citation metadata). It is in the CKEditor5 package.

Use it to add citations in the editor. It is a content-editing/CKEditor feature; it inserts citation markup
into content — ensure the text format's allowed tags permit that markup and restrict the plugin to trusted
formats. It has no access-control role. Enable it in the CKEditor 5 toolbar for a format.
