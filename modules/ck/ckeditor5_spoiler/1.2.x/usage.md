CKEditor5 Spoiler extends CKEditor 5 with a spoiler plugin, letting editors mark content as a hide/reveal spoiler.

---

CKEditor5 Spoiler adds a spoiler plugin to CKEditor 5 — letting editors mark a section of content as a
spoiler (initially hidden, revealed on click), useful for hiding plot details, answers or optional content
within rich text. It depends on core CKEditor 5.

Use it to add hide/reveal spoiler content in the editor. It is a content-editing/CKEditor feature; the
spoiler becomes markup in the content, so ensure the text format's allowed tags/attributes permit the
spoiler markup (so it survives filtering). No access-control role — the spoiler is a display toggle, not
access control (the hidden content is still in the page source). Add the spoiler tool to the CKEditor 5
toolbar.
