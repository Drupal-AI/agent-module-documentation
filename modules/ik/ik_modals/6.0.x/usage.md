Modal Management Module (ik_modals) provides modal content management.

---

Modal Management Module (ik_modals) provides modal content management — letting site builders create and
control modals/dialogs (their content, triggers and behaviour) as managed configuration, so popups/modals can
be maintained centrally. It is configured at `ik_modals.settings` and provides its own permissions, in the IK
package.

Use it to manage site modals. It is a content-display/UI feature; modal content is admin-authored content
shown to visitors, and it has no access-control role. Gate who can manage modals with its permissions.
Configure the modals.
