TAPIS Systems integrates TAPIS systems (compute/storage systems) into Drupal.

---

TAPIS Systems **integrates TAPIS systems into Drupal** — managing TAPIS "systems" (the compute/storage
resources jobs run on and read/write from) so a Drupal science gateway can configure and reference them. It depends
on the TAPIS Tenant/Auth modules, Storage, Field Group, Inline Entity Form, Node, Views and others, and provides
its own permissions, in the Tapis package.

Use it to manage TAPIS compute/storage systems. It is a research-computing integration. Security/data handling: it
**calls the external TAPIS API** (egress) via TAPIS Auth, and TAPIS systems reference **storage/compute credentials**
— keep those secured (via TAPIS Auth/Key) and connect over HTTPS. It has its own permissions. Configure the TAPIS
systems.
