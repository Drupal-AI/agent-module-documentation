Add a numeric proportion/weight to taxonomy terms.

---

Taxonomy proportion allows adding a proportion to taxonomy terms — attaching a numeric proportion/percentage value to each term reference (e.g. an ingredient's share of a recipe, or a tag's relevance weight), so term references carry a quantitative weighting. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
