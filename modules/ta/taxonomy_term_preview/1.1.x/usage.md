Taxonomy Term Preview extends the taxonomy term form to handle previewing the term.

---

Taxonomy Term Preview extends the **taxonomy term edit form with a preview** — so editors can preview how
a term will look before saving, matching the preview experience nodes already have. It depends on core
Taxonomy, in the taxonomy package.

Use it to let editors preview terms. It is a content-editing feature; preview respects the term's normal
access/rendering and it has no access-control role. Enable it to add the preview button to term forms.
