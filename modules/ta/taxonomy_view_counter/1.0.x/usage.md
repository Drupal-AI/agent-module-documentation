Count taxonomy term-page views and expose the count to Views.

---

Taxonomy View Counter counts total views of taxonomy term pages and displays that in Views — recording how many times each term's page has been viewed and exposing the count as a Views field, so popular terms can be surfaced/sorted. Depends on core `views` and `taxonomy`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
