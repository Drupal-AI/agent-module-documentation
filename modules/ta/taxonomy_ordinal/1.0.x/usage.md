Taxonomy Ordinal provides hierarchical inherited numbering of taxonomy terms.

---

Taxonomy Ordinal **numbers taxonomy terms hierarchically** — assigning inherited ordinal numbers (e.g. 1, 1.1,
1.2) to terms and the entities assigned to them, for structured/outline-style numbering. It depends on core
Taxonomy.

Use it for outline-numbered taxonomies. It is a content/site-building feature; it computes ordinal numbers and has
no access-control role. Configure the ordinal numbering.
