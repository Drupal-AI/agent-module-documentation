Tally provides a field that counts references per entity.

---

Tally adds a field that calculates a simple count per referenced entity — so an entity can display a tally (count) of how many times it's referenced or how many related items it has, useful for showing counts (votes, references, related content) without custom code.

It's a field/computed-value provider with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
