Tarte au citron AddToAny gates the AddToAny sharing buttons behind Tarte au citron consent.

---

Tarte au citron AddToAny **gates AddToAny behind cookie consent** — an integration that only loads the
AddToAny social-sharing buttons after the visitor has granted the matching Tarte au citron consent, for GDPR
compliance. It depends on the Tarte au citron and AddToAny modules, in the GDPR package.

Use it to make AddToAny consent-aware. It is a privacy/compliance feature; it defers loading the third-party
AddToAny script until consent and has no access-control role. Configure the consent gating.
