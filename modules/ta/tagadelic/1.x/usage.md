Tagadelic builds weighted tag clouds from taxonomy or any weighted list, sizing terms by frequency.

---

The tag cloud — terms sized by how often they are used — is a classic content-discovery widget, and Tagadelic is its long-standing Drupal implementation, building weighted clouds from taxonomy or any weighted list. It is a display module with no security surface. Tag clouds have fallen somewhat out of fashion and can be a weak navigation aid on large vocabularies, so consider whether a cloud or a more structured facet suits your content, but where a cloud is wanted this builds it.
