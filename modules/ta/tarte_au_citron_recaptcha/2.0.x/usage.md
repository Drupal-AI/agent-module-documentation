A reCAPTCHA consent plugin for the Tarte au citron cookie manager.

---

Tarte au citron Recaptcha is a reCAPTCHA plugin for the Tarte au citron module — so Google reCAPTCHA is loaded only after the visitor consents to it via the Tarte au citron cookie-consent banner, keeping reCAPTCHA's third-party cookies GDPR-compliant. Depends on `tarte_au_citron` and `recaptcha`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
