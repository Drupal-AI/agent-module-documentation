Tab Title Attention animates the browser-tab title to grab attention on inactive tabs.

---

Tab Title Attention **animates the tab title on inactive tabs** — when the visitor switches away, it animates
the browser tab's title (e.g. scrolling text or an alert) to draw attention back. It provides its own permissions,
in the User interface package.

Use it to re-engage visitors on inactive tabs. It is a purely front-end/UX feature; it changes the tab title and
has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure the tab-title animation.
