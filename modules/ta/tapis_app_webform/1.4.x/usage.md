Tapis App Webform integrates Webform with TAPIS apps.

---

Tapis App Webform **integrates Webform with TAPIS apps** — connecting Drupal Webforms to TAPIS applications so
form submissions can drive TAPIS app inputs/job parameters in a science gateway. It depends on the Webform and
Webform UI modules and provides its own permissions, in the Tapis package.

Use it to build TAPIS app forms with Webform. It is a research-computing/forms integration. Data-handling note:
form submissions feed **TAPIS app/job parameters** and may be sent to the TAPIS API (egress via the TAPIS modules)
— handle submission data appropriately. It has its own permissions. Configure the Webform-to-TAPIS mapping.
