Taxonomy Content Report generates content-insight reports filterable by a taxonomy tree.

---

Taxonomy Content Report **generates content reports by taxonomy** — showing content insights filterable by a
hierarchical taxonomy tree, integrating with Views for customizable content tables. It depends on core Taxonomy,
Node, Views and Views UI, and provides its own permissions, in the Reporting package.

Use it for taxonomy-scoped content reporting. It is an administration/reporting feature; reports aggregate content
(admin-facing) and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. Note: reports surface content — gate the
report to appropriate roles. Configure the report.
