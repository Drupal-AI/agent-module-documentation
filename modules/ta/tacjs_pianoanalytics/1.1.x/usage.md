A Piano Analytics plugin for the TacJS (Tarte au citron) consent manager.

---

Piano Analytics for TacJS is a Piano Analytics extension for the TacJS module (a Tarte au citron consent manager) — so Piano Analytics tracking is loaded only after the visitor consents via the TacJS cookie-consent UI, keeping analytics GDPR-compliant. Depends on `pianoanalytics` and `tacjs`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
