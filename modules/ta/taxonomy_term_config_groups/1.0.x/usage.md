Taxonomy Term Config Groups enables per-vocabulary grouping functionality for terms.

---

Taxonomy Term Config Groups **enables term grouping per vocabulary** — providing settings to turn on grouping
functionality for taxonomy terms on a per-vocabulary basis, for organizing terms into groups. It depends on core
Taxonomy and Field, and provides its own permissions.

Use it to group terms within vocabularies. It is a taxonomy/site-building feature; it configures grouping and has
no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure grouping per vocabulary.
