Concord Cookie Consent embeds the Concord cookie-consent banner for GDPR/consent management.

---

Concord Cookie Consent integrates the Concord cookie-consent banner, presenting visitors with a consent prompt for cookies/tracking to support GDPR and similar privacy requirements. Configuration is gated by a settings permission.

The banner loads Concord's third-party consent script; review data-flow and ensure it gates the trackers you intend. Gated by `concord_cookie_consent_settings`. Supports Drupal 8+.
