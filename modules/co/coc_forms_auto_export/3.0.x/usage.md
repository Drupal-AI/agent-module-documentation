Webform Auto Exports provides automatic export of Drupal Webform submission results (e.g. to files) on a schedule.

---

Webform Auto Exports (coc_forms_auto_export) automatically exports Webform submission results —
generating exports of form submissions (typically to files, on a schedule) so submission data is regularly
captured/delivered without manual export. It depends on the Webform and Webform UI modules.

Use it to automate delivery of webform submissions. The security/privacy-relevant point is significant:
webform submissions frequently contain **personal data** (names, emails, messages, uploaded files), so the
exported files are sensitive — ensure the export destination is access-controlled (not web-accessible),
stored securely, and handled per your data-protection/retention obligations. It is an import/export feature
producing submission exports; it has no access-control role but produces sensitive output. Configure which
forms are exported and the destination.
