Commerce Recurring Shipping makes it possible to add shipping to subscriptions.

---

Commerce Recurring Shipping **adds shipping to Commerce Recurring subscriptions** — so recurring/
subscription orders can include and charge shipping on each renewal, bridging Commerce Recurring and Commerce
Shipping. It depends on both modules, in the Commerce (contrib) package.

Use it to charge shipping on subscription renewals. It is an e-commerce feature; it works within Commerce's
order/pricing and it has no access-control role. Configure shipping on recurring order types.
