Compare role permissions shows a side-by-side comparison of permissions across roles.

---

Compare role permissions provides an admin report that compares the permissions of different roles side by side, making it easy to audit which role has which permission and spot over-privileged roles or gaps. It's a read-only administration/security aid.

Access is gated by `compare role permissions`. It reads role/permission config but does not change it. Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
