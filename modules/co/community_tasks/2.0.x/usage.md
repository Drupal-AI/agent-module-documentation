Community Tasks lets volunteers sign up for listed tasks and shows completed ones on their profile.

---

Community Tasks provides a volunteer task system: tasks are listed, community members sign up to take them on, and completed tasks appear on the volunteer's user profile — encouraging participation and recognising contribution. It's aimed at community, nonprofit and membership sites.

It builds on core `comment`, `datetime`, `options`, `text`, and `views`. A user-engagement feature; grant sign-up/management appropriately. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
