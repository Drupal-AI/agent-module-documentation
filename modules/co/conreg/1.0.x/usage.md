ConReg (Convention Registration) provides a simple convention registration form and management.

---

ConReg **provides a simple convention registration** — a form and management for registering attendees to a
convention/event (memberships, attendee details, and typically payment). It provides its own permissions.

Use it to run convention sign-ups. It is a user-engagement/event feature. Data-handling note: it collects
**attendee personal data** (and, if it takes payment, is a financial flow) — expose registrations only to
organizers, secure any payment integration, and handle PII per your privacy policy. It has its own permissions for
management. Configure the convention registration.
