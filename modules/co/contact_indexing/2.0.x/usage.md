Adds a robots meta tag to contact forms to control search-engine indexing.

---

Contact Form Indexing adds a `robots` meta tag with `index, follow` to selected contact form pages — letting a site opt specific contact forms into search-engine indexing and link crawling per form, which core doesn't expose out of the box. It's a small SEO/crawler-control add-on. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
