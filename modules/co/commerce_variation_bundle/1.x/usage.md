Commerce Variation Bundle groups multiple product variations into a bundle sold as a unit, with an attributes submodule.

---

Selling a set of variations together — a bundle deal, a kit — needs the variations grouped and priced as a unit. Commerce Variation Bundle provides that, with a `commerce_variation_bundle_attributes` submodule. It is a Commerce product-modelling feature with no unusual security surface. Confirm the bundle's pricing and stock behaviour match intent (a bundle's availability depends on its component variations).
