Commerce Zero-Out Tax Promotion adds a promotion offer that zeroes out tax by removing tax adjustments from the order.

---

Commerce Zero-Out Tax Promotion **adds a promotion offer that zeroes out tax** — a Commerce promotion offer
plugin that removes tax adjustments from an order (e.g. for tax-exempt customers or promotions). It works with
Drupal Commerce promotions.

Use it to create tax-zeroing promotions. It is an e-commerce/pricing feature; it removes tax adjustments per
promotion rules and has no access-control role. Note: zeroing tax has **compliance implications** — apply it only
where tax exemption is legitimately warranted. Configure the zero-out-tax promotion.
