Contact Permissions provides permissions for having a personal contact form, letting you control which roles get a personal contact form.

---

Contact Permissions adds permission control over personal contact forms — core lets users enable a
personal contact form on their profile (so others can email them), and this module lets you govern that by
permission, controlling which roles may have/use a personal contact form. It depends on core Contact.

Use it to restrict personal contact forms to certain roles (e.g. staff only), rather than the all-or-
nothing core behaviour. It is an access-control feature adding permissions around the personal-contact-form
capability; assign the permissions to the roles that should have personal contact forms. Verify the
assignment matches your intent (who should be contactable via a personal form).
