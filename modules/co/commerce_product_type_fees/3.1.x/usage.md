Commerce Product Type Fees provides configurable custom fees per product type.

---

Commerce Product Type Fees adds **configurable custom fees per Commerce product type** — so an extra
fee (handling, deposit, surcharge) can be automatically applied to orders containing products of a given
product type. It depends on Commerce, Commerce Order and Commerce Product, in the Commerce package.

Use it to apply per-product-type fees to orders. It is an e-commerce/pricing feature; fees are applied via
Commerce's order-processing (server-side, authoritative) and it has no access-control role. Configure the fees
per product type.
