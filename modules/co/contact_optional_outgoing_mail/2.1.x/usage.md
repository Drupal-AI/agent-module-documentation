Makes the 'recipient' field for contact forms optional.

---

Contact Optional Outgoing Mail makes the 'recipient' field for contact forms optional — so a contact form can be saved/used without a recipient email (e.g. when submissions are only stored or handled by another module), rather than core requiring at least one recipient. It's a small contact-form tweak. Depends on core `contact`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
