Commerce Product Availability provides product variation availability logic, controlling when variations are available for purchase.

---

Commerce Product Availability provides availability logic for Drupal Commerce product variations —
determining when a variation is available for purchase (based on stock, dates, or other rules), so
unavailable variations are handled appropriately in the store. It ships a
`commerce_product_availability_webform_request` submodule (request-availability via webform), is configured
at `commerce_product_availability.settings`, and provides its own permissions.

Use it to control product-variation availability. It is an e-commerce feature governing purchasability; the
availability logic affects whether items can be bought, not content access. Configure the availability rules
and, if used, the webform request flow.
