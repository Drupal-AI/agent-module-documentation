Commerce Profile Inline Form Radios shows saved profiles as selectable radio buttons at checkout.

---

Commerce Profile Inline Form Radios adds a Commerce inline form that renders a customer's saved profiles (billing/shipping addresses) as radio buttons, so returning customers can pick an existing profile quickly instead of re-entering details. It's a checkout-UX refinement.

It's a checkout widget with no access role of its own. Depends on Commerce `commerce_order`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
