Commerce Stock Notifications allows users to submit a request to be notified when a product comes back in stock, integrating with Commerce Stock.

---

Commerce Stock Notifications lets shoppers sign up to be notified when an out-of-stock product
becomes available again — a "notify me when back in stock" feature. When stock is replenished (tracked
via the Commerce Stock module), the module notifies users who registered interest. It depends on
Commerce (core, cart, product), Commerce Stock and its field, and Token; requires PHP 7.0+ and provides
its own permissions.

Use it to capture demand for out-of-stock items and re-engage customers when products return. It
collects users' contact details (email) for the notification, so handle that data with appropriate
consent/privacy, and the notification sends are driven by stock changes. It provides permissions to
manage the feature. Configure the notification messaging (Token-based) and which products participate.
