Consent Management creates data policies and manages user consent.

---

Consent Management **creates data policies and manages user consent** to them — presenting policy documents
(e.g. privacy/terms), recording each user's consent (and version), and re-prompting when a policy changes, for
GDPR-style consent tracking. It depends on core Block and Path Alias, provides its own permissions.

Use it to capture and record user consent to policies. It is a privacy/compliance feature. Data-handling note:
it stores **consent records tied to users** (personal data — retain and expose them per your privacy policy),
and the consent record is the compliance artifact, so protect and audit it. It gates its admin functions by its
permission and otherwise layers on normal access. Configure the data policies and consent prompts.
