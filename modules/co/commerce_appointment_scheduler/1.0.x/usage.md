Commerce Appointment Scheduler adds bookable time-slots to Commerce products and order items.

---

Commerce Appointment Scheduler brings slot-based appointment booking into Drupal Commerce: product variations gain schedulable time slots, and order items capture the chosen appointment — suitable for selling services, consultations, or classes where a customer books a specific time.

Permissions cover administration (`administer commerce appointment scheduler`) and viewing bookings (`view commerce appointment bookings`). Depends on core `datetime`/`field`/`image` and Commerce (`commerce`, `commerce_cart`, `commerce_order`, `commerce_product`); requires Drupal 11.
