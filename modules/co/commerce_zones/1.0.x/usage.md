Commerce Zones provides territorial groupings based on countries, subdivisions and postal codes, for use in Commerce shipping, tax and other zone-based logic.

---

Commerce Zones provides reusable territorial zones — groupings of geography defined by countries,
subdivisions (states/provinces) and postal codes — that other Commerce features can use to apply
zone-based logic, such as shipping rates, tax rules or availability. It depends on the Address and
Commerce modules and ships example and shipping submodules; it provides its own permissions.

Use it to define the regions your store treats specially (shipping zones, tax jurisdictions). It is an
e-commerce configuration primitive; zones are admin-defined and consumed by shipping/tax logic, with no
access-control role. Configure the zones and reference them from the relevant Commerce features.
