Commerce Checkbox Checkout Pane adds a custom checkbox pane (e.g. terms acceptance) to checkout.

---

Commerce Checkbox Checkout Pane adds a configurable checkbox pane to the Drupal Commerce checkout flow — typically for terms-and-conditions acceptance, opt-ins, or required acknowledgements the customer must check before completing an order. The pane's label/requirement are configurable.

It's a checkout-UX module with no access role of its own. Depends on Commerce `commerce_checkout`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
