Content Metrics shows charts of content activity (e.g. items created per month over the past year).

---

Content Metrics **shows charts of content activity** — visualizing metrics like how many items were created
per month over the past 12 months, for editorial insight. It depends on the ChartJS API module.

Use it to see content-creation trends. It is a statistics/reporting feature; it aggregates content metadata into
charts (admin-facing) and has no access-control role. Note: the reports summarize site content, so expose the
dashboard only to appropriate roles. View the content-metrics charts.
