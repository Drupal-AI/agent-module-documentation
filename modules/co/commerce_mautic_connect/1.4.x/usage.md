Commerce Mautic Connect syncs Commerce data to Mautic for marketing automation.

---

Commerce Mautic Connect bridges Drupal Commerce and Mautic (open-source marketing automation), pushing commerce events and data — abandoned carts, magic-link flows, and RFM (recency/frequency/monetary) segmentation — to Mautic so marketers can automate campaigns based on shopping behaviour.

It sends customer/cart data to Mautic via the advanced_mautic_integration module; review data-flow/privacy and keep Mautic API credentials secure (env-backed). Depends on Commerce `commerce_cart` and `advanced_mautic_integration`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
