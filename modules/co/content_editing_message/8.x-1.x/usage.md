Content Editing Messages shows a message to content editors when editing an entity.

---

Content Editing Messages displays a configurable message to editors when they edit an entity — showing
guidance/warnings (e.g. "This page is used on the homepage — edit carefully") on the entity edit form, to
inform editors of context or caveats. It is configured at the `content_editing_message` collection and
provides its own permissions.

Use it to surface editorial guidance on edit forms. It is a content-editing/UX feature; the messages are
admin-authored and it has no access-control role. Gate who can configure messages with its permissions.
Configure the editing messages.
