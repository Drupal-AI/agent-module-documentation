Commerce Limit Subscriptions limits how many active subscriptions a customer can hold.

---

Commerce Limit Subscriptions **limits subscriptions** — enforcing a cap on how many (recurring) subscriptions
a customer may hold/purchase, using Commerce Recurring and the cart. It depends on Commerce, Commerce Recurring and
Commerce Cart.

Use it to cap subscriptions per customer. It is an e-commerce/business-rule feature; it constrains cart/checkout
based on subscription counts and has no access-control role. Configure the subscription limit.
