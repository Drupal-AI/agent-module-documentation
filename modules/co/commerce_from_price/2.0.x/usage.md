Commerce From Price provides a product variation field formatter to display the lowest variation price.

---

Commerce From Price provides a field formatter for Drupal Commerce products that shows the **lowest**
variation price — displaying a "from £X" style price on a product with multiple variations, so listings show
the starting price. It depends on Drupal Commerce, in the Commerce (contrib) package.

Use it to show "from" prices on multi-variation products. It is an e-commerce/display feature computing the
minimum variation price; the price reflects product data (respecting its access) and it has no access-control
role. Select the formatter on the product's variations field.
