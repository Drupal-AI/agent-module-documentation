Contact Storage Disable Mail adds the ability to disable email notifications on a per-contact-form basis.

---

Contact Storage Disable Mail extends Contact Storage — letting you **disable the email notification** for
a specific contact form (so a form stores submissions without also emailing them), useful when submissions
are only reviewed in the database. It depends on the Contact Storage module.

Use it where a contact form should store but not email. It is a forms/contact feature affecting notification
delivery; submissions are still stored (governed by contact/contact-storage access) and it has no
access-control role. Configure the disable-mail option per contact form.
