Commerce Product Reservation lets customers reserve products (hold stock) in Commerce.

---

Commerce Product Reservation allows users to reserve products — adding a reservation flow so a customer can hold/reserve a product (and its stock) rather than (or before) buying it outright, useful for made-to-order, limited-stock, or appointment-style commerce.

It builds on Commerce cart/checkout and ships an `add_to_cart` order-item form mode (from Commerce Cart). Store nothing sensitive of its own. Depends on `commerce_product`, `commerce_cart`, and `commerce_ajax_cart_message`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
