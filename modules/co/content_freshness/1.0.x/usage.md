A color-coded badge showing how fresh or stale content is by last-updated date.

---

Content Freshness Indicator displays a visual color-coded badge on content showing how fresh or stale it is based on the last updated date, with configurable thresholds per content type — so editors can spot content that needs review at a glance. Depends on core `node`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
