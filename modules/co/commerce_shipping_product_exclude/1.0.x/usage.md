Commerce Shipping Product Exclude lets products/variations forbid particular shipping methods.

---

Commerce Shipping Product Exclude adds a Commerce product/variation field type and a Commerce shipping condition which together exclude specific shipping methods not allowed for a product or variation — so a store can mark that certain products cannot ship via certain methods (e.g. hazardous items, oversized goods), and the condition removes those methods at checkout when such a product is in the cart.

It's a shipping-rules enhancement with no payment or access role of its own. Depends on `commerce` and `commerce_shipping`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
