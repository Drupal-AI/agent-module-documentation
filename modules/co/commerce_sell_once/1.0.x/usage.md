Commerce Sell Once provides a stock service capping a product to a single sale.

---

Commerce Sell Once is a Commerce Stock service that enforces selling a product exactly once — after the single unit sells, the product becomes unavailable. It suits unique items (one-off artworks, single event tickets, one-of-a-kind inventory) where stock is strictly one.

It plugs into the Commerce Stock framework and has no access role of its own. Depends on `commerce_stock`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
