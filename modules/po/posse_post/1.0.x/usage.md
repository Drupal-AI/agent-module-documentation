POSSE Post publishes content on your own site and syndicates it to social platforms.

---

POSSE Post **syndicates content to social platforms** — implementing POSSE (Publish on your Own Site, Syndicate
Elsewhere): content is published on the Drupal site and automatically cross-posted to configured social networks. It
depends on core System, User and Node, and provides its own permissions, in the Social package.

Use it to auto-syndicate content to social. It is a social/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **posts
content to external social platforms via their APIs** (egress) and authenticates with **per-platform OAuth
credentials/tokens** (store as secrets — env/Key — over HTTPS; a social token can post as your account). It has no
access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the social credentials.
