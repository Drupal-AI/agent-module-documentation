Generate different types of poster sliders.

---

Poster Slider lets you generate different types of poster sliders — configurable image/poster slideshows (hero banners, promo carousels) with various layouts, for showcasing featured content on a page. Depends on core `options` and `user`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
