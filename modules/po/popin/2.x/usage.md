Popin lets a popin (modal popup) be displayed on the website, for announcements, promotions or consent-style prompts.

---

A popin/modal for an announcement, a promotion, or a prompt is a common marketing/UX element. Popin lets one be configured and displayed. It is a display feature. Content in the popin is admin-configured, so no unusual security surface. Two UX/ethics notes rather than security: a popin that appears before consent for tracking should not itself set tracking cookies, and intrusive popins harm UX — but these are configuration/design choices. Confirm the popin's display conditions and content are appropriate.
