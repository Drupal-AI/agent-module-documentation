Convert text to speech with Amazon Polly.

---

Polly integrates with Amazon Polly — converting text (e.g. an article body) to natural-sounding speech audio via AWS Polly, so a site can offer 'listen to this page' audio for accessibility and convenience.

The AWS credentials are handled via the `aws` module (store them securely, env-backed). Depends on core `datetime`, `file`, `link`, `options`, and `aws`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
