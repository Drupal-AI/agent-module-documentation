WissKI Edit Layout replaces the default entity edit form with a denser layout.

---

A WissKI record modelled against a serious ontology has a lot of fields, and Drupal's default form layout — one field per row, generously spaced — turns that into a form several screens long. A cataloguer working through hundreds of objects spends a measurable part of their day scrolling.

This submodule replaces that layout with a more space-efficient one, which is a small change with a real effect on the throughput of the people who actually use the system.

It is worth noting as a category of module that specialist systems need and general ones do not. A content editor writing three articles a week does not care about form density; a cataloguer entering their four-hundredth object does. Optimising for the second is a different design problem, and a project supporting cataloguing work should treat form ergonomics as a requirement rather than a preference.

Check it against the fields your pathbuilder actually produces — a denser layout helps most where there are many short fields and can hurt where there are few long ones.
