Create HLS streaming audio and video derivatives for Islandora.

---

Islandora HLS creates streaming audio and video derivatives — generating HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) versions of Islandora media so large audio/video repository objects stream adaptively to the browser instead of downloading in full. Depends on `islandora`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
