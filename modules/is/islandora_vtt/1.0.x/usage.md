Generate and display WebVTT captions for Islandora audio/video.

---

Islandora VTT supports generating and displaying VTT files for audio and video — producing WebVTT caption/transcript tracks (via text extraction) for Islandora media and displaying them with the player, improving accessibility of A/V repository objects. Depends on `islandora`, `context`, and `islandora_text_extraction`; supports Drupal 10.1+, 11, and 12.
