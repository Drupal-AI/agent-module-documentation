Assign administrators to parent items to easily assign editors to Islandora objects.

---

Islandora Access lets you assign administrators to parent items to easily assign editors to Islandora objects — so access/editing rights for repository objects can be managed by delegating an admin at a parent (collection) level who then assigns editors, simplifying access administration in large Islandora repositories. Supports Drupal 11.1+.
