A Seers cookie-consent banner for GDPR/ePrivacy/CCPA compliance.

---

Seers Cookie Consent Banner (Privacy Policy) fulfils the requirements of transparency, consent and documentation under the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR — showing a Seers-powered cookie consent banner that records visitor consent for cookies/trackers (GDPR/CCPA).

Connect a Seers account (store any credentials securely, env-backed); the banner/consent data is handled by Seers. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
