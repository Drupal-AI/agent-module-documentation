Schema.org Hospital adds hospital/healthcare properties to Schema.org Organization JSON-LD.

---

Schema.org Hospital **adds healthcare schema properties** — extending Schema.org Organization (via Schema.org
Metatag) with hospital/healthcare-specific properties (alternateName, legal name, medical specialties, etc.) for
richer JSON-LD on healthcare sites. It depends on the Schema.org Organization module.

Use it to mark up hospitals/healthcare orgs. It is an SEO feature; JSON-LD reflects on-page content and it has no
content or access role. Configure the hospital schema.
