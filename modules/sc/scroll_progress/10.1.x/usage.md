Scroll Progress shows a progress bar indicating how far the visitor has scrolled through the page.

---

Scroll Progress displays a scroll-progress bar — a thin indicator (usually at the top of the viewport)
that fills as the visitor scrolls down the page, showing reading progress. It is configured at
`scroll_progress.settings`. This is a common UX touch on articles/long content.

Use it on long content to show reading progress. It is a front-end/UI feature; it has no content or access
role. Configure the bar's appearance and where it applies.
