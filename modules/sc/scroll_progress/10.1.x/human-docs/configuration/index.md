# Configuration

Scroll Progress is configured on its own settings form (configuration
`scroll_progress.settings`), which you can reach from the module's **Configure**
link on the **Extend** page (`/admin/modules`).

## Choose an indicator theme

The module offers **five** scroll indicator themes — pick the one that fits your
design:

- **Straight line** — a simple straight progress line.
- **Circular progress** — a circular ring that fills as you scroll.
- **Animated progress** — an animated progress bar.
- **Tooltip progress** — a tooltip-style progress indicator.
- **Bottom progress bar** — a progress bar positioned at the bottom of the page.

## Choose a colour scheme

You can set the **colour scheme** used for the indication, so the progress
indicator matches your site's palette.

## Save

Save the settings form, then reload a page and scroll to see your chosen theme and
colour in action. Because this is a front-end display feature, the change takes
effect on the pages where the indicator renders with no further setup.
