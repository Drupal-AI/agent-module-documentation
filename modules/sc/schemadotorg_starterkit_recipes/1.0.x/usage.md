A Schema.org Blueprints starter kit for a Recipe content type.

---

Schema.org Blueprints Starter Kit: Recipes creates a Schema.org Recipe type with a Recipes view — a starter kit that scaffolds a Recipe content type mapped to Schema.org Recipe (ingredients, steps, times) plus a listing view and default content, for food/recipe sites. Depends on Schema.org Blueprints submodules, `views`, and `default_content`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
