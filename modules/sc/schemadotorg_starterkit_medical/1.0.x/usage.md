A Schema.org Blueprints starter kit for medical websites.

---

Schema.org Blueprints Starter Kit: Medical creates Schema.org MedicalEntity types with views and default content — a starter kit that scaffolds medical content types (conditions, procedures, physicians, etc.) mapped to Schema.org MedicalEntity, with ready-made views and sample content, for healthcare sites. Depends on Schema.org Blueprints (many submodules), `views`, and `menu_ui`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
