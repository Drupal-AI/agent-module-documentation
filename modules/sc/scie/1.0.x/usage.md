Structured content impact scoring, governance and editorial reporting.

---

Semantic Content Impact Engine (SCIE) Analyser provides structured content impact scoring, governance controls and editorial reporting — analysing content to score its impact/quality and surfacing governance dashboards and reports so editorial teams can measure and manage content effectiveness. Depends on core `node`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
