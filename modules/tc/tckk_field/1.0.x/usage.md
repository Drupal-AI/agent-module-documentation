A field type for the Turkish national identity number (with validation).

---

T.C. Kimlik No provides a T.C. Kimlik No field — a field type for the Turkish Republic national identity number, validating the number's format/checksum so content can store a verified Turkish ID. Useful for Turkish sites collecting citizen identifiers. Depends on core `node`; supports Drupal 8 through 11.
