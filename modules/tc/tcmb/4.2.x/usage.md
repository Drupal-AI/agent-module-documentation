Show daily currency rates and gold prices from Turkey's central bank.

---

Tcmb shows daily currency rates from TCMB (Turkish Republic Central Bank), and gold prices — fetching the central bank's official daily exchange rates (and gold prices) and displaying them via blocks/a public JSON endpoint, for finance/e-commerce sites needing TRY exchange rates.

The `tcmb_json` submodule exposes the rates as a public read-only JSON feed (public reference data). Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
