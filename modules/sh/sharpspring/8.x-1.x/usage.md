SharpSpring adds a JavaScript tracking code to every page to generate sales leads in SharpSpring.

---

SharpSpring adds the SharpSpring marketing-automation tracking JavaScript to every page — so visitor
activity is tracked in SharpSpring for lead generation and marketing automation. It is configured at
`sharpspring.settings` and provides its own permissions, in the SharpSpring package.

Use it to enable SharpSpring visitor tracking. Like any third-party marketing tracker, it is a privacy/
consent consideration: it loads external JS that tracks visitors, so disclose it in your privacy policy and
gate it behind cookie/consent management (GDPR/CCPA) where required. It has no access-control role beyond its
permission (who configures it). Configure the SharpSpring account/tracking.
