SharpSpring Webforms adds SharpSpring tracking to admin-selected webforms, linking form submissions to SharpSpring.

---

SharpSpring Webforms adds SharpSpring tracking to admin-selected Webforms — so submissions of chosen
webforms are tracked/linked in SharpSpring (associating form data with SharpSpring leads). It depends on the
Webform and SharpSpring modules, provides its own permissions, in the SharpSpring package.

Use it to feed Webform submissions into SharpSpring lead tracking. Like the base SharpSpring integration it
is a privacy/consent consideration (form data flows to SharpSpring — disclose and gate behind consent where
required, and be mindful of any personal data in the forms). It has no access-control role beyond its
permission. Select which webforms are tracked.
