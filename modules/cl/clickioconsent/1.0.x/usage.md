Clickio Consent integrates the Clickio Consent CMP for GDPR/TCF cookie consent.

---

Clickio Consent **integrates the Clickio Consent management platform (CMP)** — adding Clickio's consent
banner/logic to comply with GDPR, the IAB TCF and Google Consent Mode by loading Clickio's script with your site
ID. It is in the Custom package (core 8+).

Use it to add Clickio cookie-consent. It is a privacy/integration feature. Data-handling: it **loads a
third-party script from Clickio** (external egress; the CMP runs in visitors' browsers and processes consent
data) — vet Clickio as a processor and disclose it in your privacy policy. The site ID is configuration (not a
secret). It has no access-control role. Configure the Clickio site ID.
