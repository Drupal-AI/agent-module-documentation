Vocabulary Catalog provides advanced taxonomy vocabulary browsing.

---

Vocabulary Catalog **provides an advanced taxonomy vocabulary catalog** — a richer browsing/management UI
for taxonomy vocabularies and terms (catalog views, blocks), beyond the core term listing. It depends on core
Block, Taxonomy and Serialization, provides its own permissions, in the Vocabulary Catalog package.

Use it to browse/manage taxonomy as a catalog. It is a site-structure/taxonomy feature; terms follow taxonomy
access and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the vocabulary catalog.
