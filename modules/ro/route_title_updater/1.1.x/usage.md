Dynamically override and enhance route/page titles.

---

Route Title Updater dynamically overrides and enhances route titles with SEO and intelligence features for contributed and custom modules — letting site builders change the page title of any route (including ones provided by other modules) without patching them, with token/SEO-friendly title enhancements. Depends on core `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
