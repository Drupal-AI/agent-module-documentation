A block visibility condition for top-level (parentless) taxonomy terms.

---

Root term condition provides a block condition to place a block on terms with no parent — a visibility condition that matches taxonomy term pages which are at the root of their hierarchy (no parent term), so a block can be shown only on top-level term pages. Depends on core `taxonomy`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
