Create a closed circle of links between pieces of content.

---

Round Linking creates a closed circle of links for content interlinking — automatically linking a set of content items in a ring (each links to the next, last back to the first), useful for sequential/tour-style navigation or 'next in series' interlinking. Depends on core `node` and `field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
