Broadcast short notes to the Nostr network.

---

Nostr Simple Publish broadcasts notes to the Nostr network — publishing short-form notes (e.g. on content save) to Nostr relays, so Drupal content is announced on the decentralised social protocol.

It signs events with the site's/user's Nostr private key (nsec) — store the key securely (env-backed), never committed. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
