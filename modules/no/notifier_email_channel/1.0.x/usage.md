An email channel for the Symfony Notifier integration.

---

Notifier Email Channel provides the Email Channel for Symfony Email — so Drupal's Notifier integration can deliver notifications by email via Symfony Mailer, giving Notifier an email transport alongside chat/SMS channels. Depends on `notifier`; supports Drupal 10.3+.
