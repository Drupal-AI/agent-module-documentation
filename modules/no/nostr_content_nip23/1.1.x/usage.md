Broadcast long-form Markdown content to the Nostr network.

---

Nostr long-form content NIP-23 broadcasts long-form Markdown-formatted content to the Nostr network (NIP-23) — so a Drupal article can be published as a NIP-23 long-form note on Nostr (the decentralised social protocol), reaching Nostr clients.

It signs events with the site's/user's Nostr private key (nsec) — store the key securely (env-backed), never committed. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
