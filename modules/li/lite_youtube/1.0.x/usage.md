Lite YouTube provides an alternative field formatter for YouTube videos.

---

Lite YouTube provides an **alternative, lightweight field formatter for YouTube videos** — rendering a
click-to-load "facade" (thumbnail + play button) that only loads the heavy YouTube player/iframe when the user
clicks, improving performance and reducing third-party loading until interaction. It depends on the YouTube
module, in the Media package.

Use it for faster, privacy-friendlier YouTube embeds. It is a media/performance feature; the video still loads
from **YouTube** on click (third-party — the usual embed privacy note applies, though nothing loads until the
user clicks), and it has no content or access role. Use the Lite YouTube formatter on video fields.
