Monitor environment availability and notify on outages/recovery.

---

Liveness monitors the availability of different environments and provides notifications about outages and recovery events — periodically checking whether configured environments/endpoints are up and alerting when one goes down or comes back, for uptime monitoring. It's an ops/monitoring tool. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
