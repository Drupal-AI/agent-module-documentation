IVW Tracking integrates IVW (SZM 2) audience-measurement tracking, the German standard for measuring website reach.

---

IVW Tracking integrates IVW / SZM (Szm 2) audience-measurement tracking — the German industry standard
(operated by IVW / INFOnline) for measuring website reach and usage, used by German publishers for
audited traffic figures. It adds the IVW tracking to pages, configured per content via Token-based
settings. It depends on Token and provides its own permissions.

Use it on German media sites that must report IVW-audited reach. Like all audience-measurement tracking,
it has privacy/consent implications: it sends usage data to the measurement provider — obtain appropriate
consent, integrate cookie-consent, and disclose the tracking per German/EU privacy law (TTDSG/GDPR). It
is an integration/tracking feature with no access-control role; configure the IVW identifiers/codes.
