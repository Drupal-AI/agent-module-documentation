A field for storing multiple dates via a multi-date picker.

---

Multi Dates Picker (mdp) creates an MDP field for storing multiple dates — a field type/widget (using the jQuery UI Multidatespicker) where an editor selects several individual dates on a calendar, stored as a multi-value date field, useful for event/availability dates. Depends on core `field` and `jquery_ui_datepicker`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
