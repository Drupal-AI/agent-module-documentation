MD Wordscloud renders a multi-dimensional taxonomy word cloud with D3.js.

---

MD Wordscloud displays a multi-dimensional word cloud built with D3.js — visualizing taxonomy terms (e.g. by frequency/weight across dimensions) as an interactive cloud, so sites can present tag popularity or topic maps graphically.

It's a visualization/display feature with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `taxonomy`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
