Theme region wrapper lets you customize the HTML wrapper around theme regions.

---

Theme region wrapper alters the region wrapper output of enabled themes — letting site builders customize the HTML markup that wraps each theme region (the container element/attributes around a region's blocks) without editing theme templates directly.

It's a theming utility with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
