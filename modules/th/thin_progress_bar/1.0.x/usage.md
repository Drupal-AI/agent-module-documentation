Thin Progress Bar adds a thin animated progress bar.

---

Thin Progress Bar **adds a thin animated progress bar at the top of the page** — a slim loading indicator
(NProgress-style) shown during page navigation/AJAX, for perceived-performance feedback. It is in the Custom
package.

Use it to show a page-load progress indicator. It is a front-end/UX feature affecting presentation; it has no
content or access role. Enable it to show the progress bar.
