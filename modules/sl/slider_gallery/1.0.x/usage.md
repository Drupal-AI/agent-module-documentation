Image Slider Gallery displays images in a slider/carousel gallery format.

---

Image Slider Gallery manages the presentation of images in a slider/carousel gallery format — so sites can display collections of images as an interactive sliding gallery (carousel) rather than a static grid, enhancing visual content presentation.

It's a media/display feature with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
