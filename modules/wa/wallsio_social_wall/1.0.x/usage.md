Walls.io Social Wall embeds a Walls.io social-media wall.

---

Walls.io Social Wall **embeds a Walls.io social wall** — displaying an aggregated social-media wall from the
Walls.io service (curated posts from multiple social networks) on the site. It works on core 10.3–11.

Use it to show a social wall. It is a social-media/integration feature. Security/data handling: it embeds content
from the **external Walls.io service** (egress; loads third-party scripts/content — privacy/consent) and typically
uses a **Walls.io wall ID/token** — treat any token as configuration/secret as appropriate and serve over HTTPS. It
has no access-control role. Configure the Walls.io wall.
