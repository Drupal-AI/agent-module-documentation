Migrate WAT WAT articles, organisations, paragraphs and self-tests.

---

WAT WAT synchronisation is a module for migrating articles, organisations, paragraphs, themes and self-tests (from the WAT WAT source) into Drupal — a domain-specific migration built on Migrate Plus/Tools that imports the WAT WAT content model (a Belgian sexual-health information platform) with its media, taxonomy and paragraph structures. Depends on core migrate + `migrate_plus`, `migrate_tools`, `paragraphs`, and `entity_reference_revisions`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
