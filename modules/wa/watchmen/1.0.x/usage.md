Help contributors report bugs with a moderated workflow.

---

Watchmen is a module to assist contributors in reporting bugs — providing a structured bug-report content type/workflow (with content moderation states) so community contributors can submit and triage bug reports on a Drupal site. Depends on core `user`, `text`, `options`, `workflows`, and `content_moderation`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
