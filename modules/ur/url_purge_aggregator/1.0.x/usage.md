Aggregate related URLs for cache purging via Purge.

---

Url Purge Aggregator aggregates URLs for cache purging — so when a node is invalidated, related/derived URLs are collected and queued together for cache invalidation through Purge, ensuring all affected pages (not just the canonical one) are purged from the cache/CDN. Depends on core `node` and `purge`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
