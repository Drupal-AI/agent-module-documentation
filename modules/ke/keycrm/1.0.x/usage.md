KeyCRM integration for Drupal Commerce.

---

KeyCRM integrates KeyCRM with Drupal Commerce — syncing orders/products/customers from the store to the KeyCRM CRM/order-management platform, so a merchant manages fulfilment and customers in KeyCRM.

The KeyCRM API credentials are admin-configured and should be stored securely (env-backed), never committed. Depends on `commerce`, `commerce_product`, and `commerce_order`; supports Drupal 9 through 12.
