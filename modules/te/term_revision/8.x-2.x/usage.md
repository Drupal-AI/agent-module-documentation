Term Revision adds taxonomy term revision support.

---

Term Revision adds **revision support to taxonomy terms** — so edits to a term keep a revision history
you can view and revert, bringing terms closer to the revisioning nodes/other content entities already have.
It provides its own permissions, in the revision package.

Use it where taxonomy term changes need history/accountability. It is a content/editorial feature; who can
view or revert term revisions is governed by its permissions (grant them to trusted editors) and it has no
other access-control role. Enable it and grant the revision permissions.
