Telephone E.164 provides a telephone field that stores/validates E.164 phone numbers.

---

Telephone E.164 provides an E.164 telephone field type — storing phone numbers in the international E.164 standard format (with country code), with validation, so phone data is consistent and interoperable (for SMS, dialing, CRM sync) rather than free-form.

It's a field provider with no content or access role of its own; render output is escaped normally. Depends on core `telephone` and `field`; requires Drupal 11.3+.
