Telega provides Telegram integrations (bots, messaging).

---

Telega **provides Telegram integrations** — connecting Drupal to Telegram for bot messaging/notifications and
related Telegram features. It provides its own permissions, in the Telega package.

Use it to integrate Telegram. It is an integration/messaging feature. Security/data handling: it authenticates with
a **Telegram bot token** (a powerful secret — anyone with it controls your bot; store as a secret — env/Key — never
commit it) and **sends/receives messages via Telegram** (egress; and if it exposes a webhook to receive Telegram
updates, verify those requests come from Telegram). It has its own permissions. Configure the Telegram bot token.
