A Views exposed filter for year/month archives.

---

Year Month Expose Filter provides a Year Month archive filter for Views — an exposed filter that lets visitors narrow a listing to a given year and month (like a blog archive), driven by a date field, for date-archive-style navigation. Depends on core `views`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
