Multi-vendor marketplace features for MonkeysCommerce.

---

MKC Marketplace turns MonkeysCommerce into a multi-vendor marketplace — vendors, commissions, payouts, and split orders — so multiple sellers can list products, orders split across vendors, and the platform tracks commissions/payouts.

Vendor-admin routes are gated by `manage marketplace`/`manage vendors`/`manage payouts`/`manage disputes` permissions; the vendor storefront read endpoints are public by design. Depends on the MonkeysCommerce suite; supports Drupal 11.3+ and 12.
