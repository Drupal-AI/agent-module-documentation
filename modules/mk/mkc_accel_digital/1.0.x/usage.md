Digital-goods features for MonkeysCommerce (downloads, license keys).

---

MKC Accelerator Digital Goods is a digital-goods accelerator for MonkeysCommerce — file downloads, license keys, download management, and checkout customization — so a MonkeysCommerce store can sell digital products (software, media, licenses) with secure delivery. Depends on the MonkeysCommerce suite (`mkc_core`, `mkc_catalog`, `mkc_order`); supports Drupal 11.3+ and 12.
