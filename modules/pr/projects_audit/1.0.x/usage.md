Projects Audit provides functionality to audit your projects.

---

Projects Audit **audits the projects/modules installed on your site** — reporting on installed contrib/
custom projects (versions, status) to help developers review the codebase's dependencies and maintenance state.
It is in the Development package.

Use it to review installed projects. It is a developer/reporting tool; its report is developer-facing and it has
no content or access role. Run the projects audit.
