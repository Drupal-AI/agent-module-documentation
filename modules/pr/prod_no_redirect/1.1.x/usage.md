Prod No Redirect blocks the redirect to install.php on production environments.

---

Prod Should not Redirect to Installer (prod_no_redirect) ensures a production site never redirects to `install.php` — a safeguard against the situation where a misconfigured/uninitialized production Drupal exposes the installer (which can be an installation-hijack / information-disclosure risk). It hardens the production environment.

It's a security/operations hardening module with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
