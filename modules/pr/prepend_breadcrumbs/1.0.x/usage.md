Prepend items to the active breadcrumb trail.

---

Prepend Breadcrumbs prepends breadcrumbs to the active breadcrumb trail — so configured extra breadcrumb items (e.g. a home or section root) are added to the front of every page's breadcrumb, ensuring a consistent navigation root. Depends on `menu_breadcrumb`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
