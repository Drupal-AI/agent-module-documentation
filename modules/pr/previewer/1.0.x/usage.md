Previewer allows editors to preview content while editing, showing how it will look before saving.

---

Previewer lets editors preview content while editing — seeing how a node will render before saving,
improving the authoring feedback loop. It depends on core Node and is in the Development package.

Use it to give editors a live/near-live preview during content editing, reducing save-and-check cycles.
It is a content-editing/preview feature; the preview reflects the content being edited and respects the
viewer's access, changing the editing experience rather than content or access. Enable it for the
content types/editing workflows where preview helps.
