A JSON:API Extras enhancer that splits processed/raw text field values.

---

JSON:API Extras - Text Field Enhancer adds a field enhancer for JSON:API Extras that reshapes text-field output — exposing the processed (rendered) and raw/summary parts of a formatted text field in a clean structure for decoupled consumers. Depends on `jsonapi_extras`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
