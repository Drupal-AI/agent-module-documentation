Yandex YML generates a Yandex Market Language (YML) feed, the product data format Yandex Market consumes for e-commerce listings.

---

Yandex YML generates a Yandex Market Language (YML) feed — the XML-based product-data format that
Yandex Market (Yandex's shopping platform) ingests to list a store's products. It produces the feed
from site content/commerce data so products can be syndicated to Yandex Market with their prices,
availability and attributes.

Use it on stores targeting the Yandex Market marketplace that need to export a compliant YML product
feed. It is an export/integration feature producing a data feed; the feed is generated from product
data on the site. Ensure only intended product data is exposed in the feed (it is typically a public
endpoint consumed by Yandex), and keep the feed's field mappings aligned with the YML spec.
