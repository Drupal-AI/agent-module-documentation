MSACR reports which installed contrib modules lack security-advisory coverage.

---

Module Security Advisory Coverage Report (MSACR) audits the contrib modules used on a site and generates a report showing any that are NOT covered by Drupal's security-advisory policy (e.g. modules without a stable release / opted out of coverage) — helping operators identify modules that won't receive official security advisories, a real risk-management concern.

It's a security/operations reporting tool with no content role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
