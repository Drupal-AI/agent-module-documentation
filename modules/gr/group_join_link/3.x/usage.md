Group Join Link adds a join/leave link field for a Group, letting users join or leave a group where the group's membership rules permit.

---

Self-service group membership — a 'join this group' button — is a community need for the Group module. Group Join Link adds a join/leave link. It works within the Group module's permission model: whether a user can join is governed by the group type's membership settings and permissions, so the link surfaces an action the user is allowed to take (it does not bypass Group's join rules). Since joining a group can grant access to the group's content, confirm the group's join settings are what you intend (an open-join group means anyone can join and gain that access). The link respects Group's access; the configuration of who may join is the control.
