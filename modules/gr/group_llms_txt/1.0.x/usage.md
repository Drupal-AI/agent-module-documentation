Group LLMs.txt enables llms.txt functionality for groups.

---

Group LLMs.txt **enables llms.txt functionality for the Group module** — so each group can expose an
`llms.txt` (the emerging convention that tells AI crawlers/LLMs what content to use and how), scoped to that
group's content. It depends on the Group and LLMs.txt modules, in the Group package.

Use it to provide per-group llms.txt guidance. It is an SEO/AI-discoverability feature; the llms.txt reflects
group content (which follows Group's access) and it has no access-control role. Configure llms.txt per group.
