Hide specific group roles from users.

---

Group Hidden Role adds an option to hide group roles from users — so certain Group roles (e.g. internal/system roles) don't appear in group membership UIs/selects, keeping the visible role list clean and preventing confusion. Depends on `group`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
