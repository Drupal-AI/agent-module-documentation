Groups dblog errors together into issues.

---

Grouper groups dblog errors together into issues — deduplicating and aggregating similar watchdog error log entries into a single 'issue' (with counts/occurrences), so developers can see distinct problems rather than a flood of repeated log lines. Depends on core `node` and `dblog`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
