Group FolderShare ties FolderShare file/folder items to Group lifecycle events.

---

Group FolderShare integrates the FolderShare file-management module with Group — auto-creating FolderShare items (folders/files) in response to Group events (e.g. when a group is created), so each group gets its own managed file storage area. It bridges group membership to file-sharing.

Administration is gated by `administer group foldershare`; file/folder access still follows FolderShare and Group access models. Depends on `foldershare` and `group`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
