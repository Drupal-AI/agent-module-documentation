Granular Time Cache invalidates cached content on time boundaries like midnight using cache tags.

---

Granular Time Cache lets you expire cached content at granular time boundaries — for example every midnight — currently implemented via cache tags. It's useful for content whose freshness is tied to the clock (daily resets, scheduled visibility) so caches invalidate at the right moment.

It's a performance/caching utility with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 8.8+ through 11.
