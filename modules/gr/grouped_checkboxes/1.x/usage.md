Grouped checkboxes provides a widget that groups checkboxes by their parents, useful for grouping terms into their vocabularies.

---

Grouped checkboxes provides a form widget that groups checkboxes by their parent — for example grouping
taxonomy term checkboxes under their vocabularies, so a long flat checkbox list becomes organized sections.
This improves usability when selecting from hierarchical or grouped options.

Use it on multi-select fields where grouping the checkboxes aids selection. It is a content-editing/form-
widget feature affecting how options are presented; it does not change the options, data or access. Select
the grouped-checkboxes widget on the relevant field.
