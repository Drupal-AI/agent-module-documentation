Group Content Menu Bundles integrates group_content_menu with menu_item_extras.

---

Group Content Menu Bundles bridges **Group Content Menu** and **Menu Item Extras** — so per-group menus
(Group Content Menu) can use the menu-link **bundles and fields** that Menu Item Extras provides, giving group
menus richer, fieldable menu items. It depends on Field UI, Menu Item Extras and Group Content Menu.

Use it on Group-based sites that need fieldable per-group menus. It is a site-structure/Group integration
feature; menu and group access follow those modules, and it has no access-control role of its own. Enable it
to use menu bundles in group menus.
