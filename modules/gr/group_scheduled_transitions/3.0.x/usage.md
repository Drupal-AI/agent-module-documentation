Group Scheduled Transitions enables scheduled transitions to be used by group members.

---

Group Scheduled Transitions bridges the **Group** module and **Scheduled Transitions** — letting group
members schedule future content-moderation state changes (e.g. auto-publish/unpublish) on group content,
governed by group permissions. It depends on Group and Scheduled Transitions, in the Group package.

Use it on Group-based sites that need scheduled publishing per group. It is an editorial-workflow/Group
integration feature; who may schedule transitions is governed by **group permissions** (grant appropriately),
and it has no access-control role of its own. Enable it and grant the group scheduling permissions.
