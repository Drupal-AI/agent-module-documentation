Group Computed Field adds a computed field on entities that references the group(s) they belong to, for the Group module.

---

Group Computed Field adds a computed field to entities that references the group(s) the entity belongs
to — so, on a Group-based site, content/entities expose their owning group(s) as a field (computed at
runtime from group relationships) for use in Views, displays or logic. It depends on the Group module.

Use it to surface group membership of content as a field (e.g. to display or filter by group). It is a
fields/access-adjacent feature reflecting Group relationships; the computed value reflects existing group
memberships and it does not itself grant access (Group's access controls still govern). Configure the
computed field where group context is needed.
