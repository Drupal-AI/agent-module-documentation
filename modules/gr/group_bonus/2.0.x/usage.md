Group Bonus provides extra convenience features on top of the Group module.

---

Group Bonus adds a collection of bonus features to the Group module — convenience enhancements for working with groups and group content (e.g. helper routes like `/node/{node}/group`, additional UI/behaviors). It extends Group without changing its core access model.

Group access still follows the Group module's membership/permission model. Depends on `group`; supports Drupal 8 through 11.
