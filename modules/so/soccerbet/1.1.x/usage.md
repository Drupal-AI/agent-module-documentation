A football prediction/tipping game with tournaments, leaderboards and payments.

---

Soccer Bet is a football tipping game (Fußball-Tippspiel) with tournaments, rankings and payment management — letting a community predict match results across tournaments, compete on leaderboards, and manage participation payments, as an in-Drupal prediction-pool application.

Any payment/participation handling is admin-configured. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
