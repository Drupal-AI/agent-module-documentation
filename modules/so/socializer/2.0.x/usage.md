Socializer shows social media links in a block.

---

Socializer **shows social-media links in a block** — displaying configurable share/follow links to your
social profiles (or share-this-page links) in a placeable block. It provides its own permissions, in the Other
package.

Use it to add social links to the site. It is a content-display/user-engagement feature; links are
admin-configured and it has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure and place the social
block.
