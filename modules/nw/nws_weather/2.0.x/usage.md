Display US National Weather Service forecast data.

---

NWS Weather displays National Weather Service forecast data — fetching forecasts from the US NWS API for a configured location and rendering them (current conditions, multi-day forecast) as a block, for weather display on a site. Supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
