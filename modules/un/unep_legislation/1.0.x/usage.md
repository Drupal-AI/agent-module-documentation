Manage UNEP environmental-legislation content.

---

UNEP Legislation is a Drupal module for managing UNEP legislation — providing the content model (types, field groups, language handling and translation) for cataloguing UN Environment Programme environmental legislation records, for environmental-law/reference sites. Depends on core `node`, `field_group`, `languagefield`, and `content_translation`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
