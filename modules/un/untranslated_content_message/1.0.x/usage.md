Untranslated content message displays a notice when content is shown untranslated.

---

Untranslated content message provides a block that informs visitors that the content they're viewing is untranslated — shown in a fallback language rather than their selected language. It helps multilingual sites communicate when a translation isn't available.

It's a multilingual/UX block with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `language` and `block`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
