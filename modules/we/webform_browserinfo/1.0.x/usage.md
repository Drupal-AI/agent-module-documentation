Webform Browser Info provides a hidden Webform element for browser info.

---

Webform Browser Info provides a **hidden Webform element that captures the submitter's browser/device
information** — recording data such as user-agent, screen/browser details into the submission, for
diagnostics/analytics. It depends on the Webform module, in the Webform package.

Use it to record browser context with submissions. It is a forms feature. Privacy/data handling: browser/device
details are **personal data** and can contribute to **fingerprinting** — collect only what you need, disclose it
in your privacy policy, and apply your submission retention/access policy (the captured info lives in the
submission alongside other data). It has no access-control role. Add the element to a webform.
