WebReleases provides product and release information.

---

WebReleases **provides product and release information content** — content types/config for publishing
products and their releases/changelogs (part of the `web*` suite), targeting core 11.4.

Use it to publish product/release info. It is a content/site-building convenience; it installs config that
follows normal access, and it has no access-control role. Enable it to add the product/release types.
