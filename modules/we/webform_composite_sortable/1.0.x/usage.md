Webform Composite Sortable lets editors drag-reorder sub-elements within webform composites.

---

Webform Composite Sortable adds draggable sorting (weights) for Webform composite sub-elements in the form builder — so when building a composite element (a group of sub-fields), the sub-elements can be reordered by drag-and-drop instead of manually setting weights, improving the composite-building experience.

It's a Webform builder UX enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `webform`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
