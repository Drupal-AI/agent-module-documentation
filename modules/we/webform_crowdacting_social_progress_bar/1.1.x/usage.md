Webform Crowdacting Social Progress Bar adds a progress bar to Webforms.

---

Webform Crowdacting Social Progress Bar **adds a social progress bar to Webforms** — displaying progress
toward a submission/participation goal (e.g. "412 of 500 people have signed") to encourage participation
(crowdacting/campaigns). It depends on the Webform and core Filter modules.

Use it to show campaign participation progress. It is a forms/user-engagement feature; the progress reflects
submission counts and it has no content or access role. Configure the progress bar and goal on a webform.
