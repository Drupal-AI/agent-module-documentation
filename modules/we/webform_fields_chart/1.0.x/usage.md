Render a Webform's fields/logic as a Mermaid chart.

---

Webform Fields Chart outputs Webforms as Mermaid charts — visualising a webform's fields and conditional logic as a Mermaid flow/diagram, so builders can review the form's structure and conditions at a glance. Depends on `webform` and `webform_conditions`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
