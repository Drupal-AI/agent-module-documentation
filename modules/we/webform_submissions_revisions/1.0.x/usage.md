Webform Submissions Revisions provides revision tracking for webform submissions.

---

Webform Submissions Revisions **adds revision tracking to Webform submissions** — keeping a history of
changes to a submission so edits can be reviewed or reverted, rather than only the latest state. It depends on
the Webform module and core User, provides its own permissions, in the Webform package.

Use it to audit changes to form submissions. It is a forms feature; access to submissions/revisions follows
Webform's own submission access plus its permission, and it has no independent access-control role.
Data-handling note: submissions (and now their revisions) may contain **PII** — apply your retention/access
policy to the revision history too. Configure revision tracking on webforms.
