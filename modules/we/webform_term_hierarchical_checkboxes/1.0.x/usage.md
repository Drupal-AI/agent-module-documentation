Webform Term Hierarchical Checkboxes shows a taxonomy tree as nested collapsible checkboxes in webforms.

---

Webform Term Hierarchical Checkboxes provides a webform element that renders taxonomy terms as collapsible hierarchical checkboxes — so a webform can present a whole vocabulary's term tree with parent/child nesting and expand/collapse, letting users select terms at any level in a structured way.

It's a Webform element with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `taxonomy` and `webform`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
