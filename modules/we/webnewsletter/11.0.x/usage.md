WebNewsletter simplifies the management of newsletters.

---

WebNewsletter **simplifies newsletter management** — handling newsletter subscriptions and sending, built on
the Webform module (part of the `web*` suite). It depends on Webform, provides its own permissions, core 11.4+.

Use it to run newsletters. It is a marketing/email feature. Security/data handling: it stores **subscriber
lists (email PII)** and **sends bulk email** — apply your privacy/consent obligations (double opt-in,
unsubscribe), protect the subscriber list, and gate sending to trusted operators (bulk email can be abused as a
spam/relay vector). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure newsletters and
subscriptions.
