Force a CAPTCHA on all webforms.

---

Webform Captcha Mandatory makes a CAPTCHA mandatory in all webforms — automatically enforcing a CAPTCHA element on every webform (rather than per-form opt-in), ensuring bot/spam protection is applied site-wide without editors having to remember to add it. Depends on `webform_ui`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
