Webform Email Blocker blocks submissions from denylisted email addresses.

---

Webform Email Blocker **blocks known-bad emails from submitting forms** — maintaining a denylist of email
addresses/domains that are rejected when submitting a webform, to cut spam and abuse. It depends on the Webform
module, provides its own permissions, in the Security package.

Use it to block spammy/abusive submitters by email. It is a spam-control feature; it validates the submitted email
against the denylist and rejects matches, and it has no broad access-control role beyond its permission. Note: an
email denylist is one anti-spam layer — pair it with CAPTCHA/honeypot/flood control. Configure the email
denylist.
