Webform to Leads extends the Webform module to capture leads.

---

Webform to Leads **extends the Webform module to capture submissions as leads** — turning form submissions
into lead records that can be stored, tracked and (potentially) routed to a CRM/sales pipeline. It is in the
Webform package.

Use it to manage form submissions as sales leads. It is a forms/marketing feature. Data-handling note: leads
contain **personal data (PII)** from submissions — apply your retention/access policy, and if it forwards leads
to an **external CRM/endpoint**, that is data egress (use a trusted endpoint over HTTPS and store any auth as a
secret). It has no access-control role. Configure the lead capture and routing.
