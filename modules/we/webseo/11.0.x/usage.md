WebSEO bundles the most needed SEO modules and configuration.

---

WebSEO **bundles commonly-needed SEO modules and configuration** — a convenience module (part of the `web*`
suite) that pulls in and configures a curated set of SEO-related contrib modules so a site starts with sensible
SEO defaults. It targets core 11.4.

Use it to bootstrap SEO on a site. It is a site-building/SEO convenience meta-module; it enables/pulls in other
modules (each with its own behaviour), so **review what it brings in** and adjust to your needs. It has no
functionality or access role of its own. Enable it to set up SEO.
