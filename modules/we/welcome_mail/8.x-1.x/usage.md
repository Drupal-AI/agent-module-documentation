Welcome Mail sends a welcome mail after a given period of time to new users.

---

Welcome Mail sends a welcome email to newly-registered users after a configured period of time (rather
than immediately) — so new users get a follow-up welcome/onboarding email some time after signing up. It
provides its own permissions, in the Other package.

Use it to send delayed welcome emails to new users. It is a user-engagement/messaging feature; the email is
admin-authored and sent to the user's address (be mindful of using the user's email/PII), and it has no
access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the welcome email and delay.
