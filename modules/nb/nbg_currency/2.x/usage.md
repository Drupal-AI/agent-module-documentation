Display National Bank of Georgia exchange rates in a block.

---

NBG Currency displays National Bank of Georgia (NBG) exchange rates in a configurable block — fetching official GEL exchange rates from the NBG and rendering them (chosen currencies, format) as a block, useful for Georgian sites. Depends on core `block`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
