Adds HTML anchors to field group elements.

---

Field group anchors (fga) adds anchors to field group elements — so each field group renders with an HTML anchor/id, enabling in-page anchor links (e.g. a table of contents or tabbed navigation) that jump to a specific field group. Depends on `field_group`; supports Drupal 9.2+, 10, and 11.
