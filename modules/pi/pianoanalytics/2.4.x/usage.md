Piano Analytics provides AT Internet Piano Analytics integration for Drupal.

---

Piano Analytics integrates **AT Internet / Piano Analytics** tracking with Drupal — adding the Piano
Analytics tracker to pages (with a `pianoanalytics_server` submodule for server-side tracking). It provides its
own permissions, in the Statistics package.

Use it to add Piano Analytics. It is a third-party analytics/integration feature. Privacy/security notes: it
enables **visitor tracking** and loads **Piano's third-party JavaScript** (client-side) and/or sends events
**server-side to Piano** — a GDPR/consent consideration (combine with your consent tooling so tracking respects
consent), and any Piano **API credentials** (server-side) should be handled as secrets. It has no access-control
role beyond its permission. Configure the Piano Analytics site ID/credentials.
