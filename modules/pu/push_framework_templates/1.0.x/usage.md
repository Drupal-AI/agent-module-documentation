Custom notification templates driven by DANSE context.

---

Push Framework Templates provides custom notification templates based on DANSE notification context — letting Push Framework render tailored notification content (per event/context supplied by DANSE) so outbound notifications can be templated rather than hard-coded. Depends on `danse` and `push_framework`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
