Publication Scheduler combines Publication Date and Scheduler for accurate scheduled publish dates.

---

Publication Scheduler makes the Publication Date and Scheduler modules work together in a better way — so scheduled content gets an accurate publication date when it goes live (rather than its creation date), improving date accuracy for scheduled publishing workflows.

Permissions cover editing the created date (`allow editing of created date`) and unhiding fields (`allow to unhide fields`). Depends on `publication_date` and `scheduler`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
