Form change warning shows an unsaved-changes warning when a form input is modified.

---

Form change warning displays a warning banner at the top of a form when any input has changed, helping users avoid navigating away and losing unsaved edits. It's a small editorial-UX safeguard.

It's a front-end form enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
