An interactive Drush console (REPL) for running queries.

---

Fox drush console produces an interactive Drush infinitive console (like the `mysql` client) — a REPL where a developer runs queries/commands interactively in a persistent session rather than one-off drush invocations, for faster exploration. It's a developer/CLI tool. Supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
