Relaunch (rl) provides A/B and multivariate testing for Drupal, serving content variants and measuring outcomes, with example, menu-link and page-title submodules.

---

Optimising content by testing variants — A/B and multivariate testing — measures which version performs better. Relaunch provides that for Drupal, with example, menu-link and page-title submodules. It serves different content variants to different visitors and tracks outcomes. The considerations are performance/UX (variant serving must not break caching or SEO — different content per visitor interacts with caching and can confuse crawlers) and privacy (assigning visitors to variants and tracking outcomes is behavioural tracking, so consider consent and what is recorded). Configure the tests carefully with those in mind. For content optimisation it is the testing framework.
