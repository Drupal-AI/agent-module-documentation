SmartLinker AI extends the AI CKEditor plugin to suggest/insert contextual links.

---

SmartLinker AI provides an additional feature in the AI CKEditor plugin, allowing editors to auto-generate contextual links within content — using AI (with AI Search) to suggest relevant internal links for the text being edited, improving internal linking and content discoverability directly in the editor.

AI suggestions run through the configured AI provider (cost) and use AI Search over indexed content; keep usage to trusted editors. Depends on `ai_ckeditor` and `ai_search`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
