AI-powered form filling from unstructured clipboard data.

---

Smart Paste provides AI-powered form filling — automatically extracting and populating form fields from unstructured clipboard data, so a user can paste a blob of text (e.g. an address or contact block) and have an AI model map it into the right form fields.

It uses the AI module's provider abstraction (credentials handled there, env-backed). Depends on `ai`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
