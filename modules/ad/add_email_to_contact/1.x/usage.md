Add Email To Contact adds the sender's email to core Contact form messages.

---

Add Email To Contact ensures emails sent via the core Contact module always include the email of the submitting user — so recipients of contact-form messages can see (and reply to) the sender's address even when core wouldn't otherwise include it, improving contact-form usability.

It's a Contact-module enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `contact`; supports Drupal 9 through 12.
