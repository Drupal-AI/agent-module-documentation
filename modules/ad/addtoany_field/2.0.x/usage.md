AddToAny Field creates a field type used for AddToAny share links, letting editors attach share buttons per entity.

---

Install AddToAny Field (requires the AddToAny module plus core node and link). Add the AddToAny field to a content type via Manage fields, then configure its formatter/widget in Manage form display and Manage display.

---

- Add an AddToAny share-links field to entities.
- Depend on the AddToAny base module.
- Depend on core node and link modules.
- Attach share buttons per content item.
- Configure the field through the standard Field UI.
- Choose services in the field/formatter settings.
- Render share links where the field is displayed.
- Provide a field type, widget and formatter.
- Let editors control share buttons per node.
- Reuse AddToAny's service configuration.
- Serve as a front-end sharing widget.
- Have no access-control role.
- Work with any fieldable entity type.
- Place buttons in the entity display.
- Support link-based share targets.
- Keep sharing on public content.
- Integrate with themes via display settings.
- Complement the site-wide AddToAny block.
