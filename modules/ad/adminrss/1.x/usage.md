Admin RSS adds a moderation/approval workflow for queued feed items.

---

Admin RSS provides moderation/approval for queue feeds — letting administrators review and approve/reject items coming through feed queues before they're published/processed, adding an editorial gate to feed ingestion.

Configuration is under `/admin/config/services/adminrss` (gated by `administer site configuration`). It's an admin/moderation tool with no front-end role of its own. Requires Drupal 9.5+.
