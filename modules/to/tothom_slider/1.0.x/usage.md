TOTHOM Slider provides accessible, configurable sliders/carousels powered by Splide.js.

---

TOTHOM Slider **provides accessible sliders/carousels** — highly configurable slide/carousel displays powered
by Splide.js, with a focus on accessibility, driven by Views. It depends on core Views and provides its own
permissions, in the Sliders package.

Use it to build accessible carousels. It is a content-display/theming feature; slides are content rendered in a
carousel and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the slider.
