Geolocation Leaflet GeoJSON adds a geojson layer for geolocation leaflet.

---

Geolocation Leaflet GeoJSON adds a **GeoJSON layer to the Geolocation module's Leaflet maps** — so a
Leaflet map (from Geolocation) can overlay GeoJSON data (boundaries, routes, custom shapes). It depends on the
Geolocation and Geolocation Leaflet modules, in the Geolocation package.

Use it to overlay GeoJSON on Leaflet maps. It is a content-display/maps feature; the GeoJSON source is
admin/developer-configured and it has no access-control role. Configure the GeoJSON layer.
