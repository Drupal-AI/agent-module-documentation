Ultimate Cron Summary adds a compact cron-health overview dashboard to Ultimate Cron.

---

Ultimate Cron Summary **adds a cron-health overview dashboard** — extending Ultimate Cron with a compact
summary so admins can assess cron job health at a glance and jump to job details. It depends on the Service and
Ultimate Cron modules.

Use it to monitor cron jobs. It is an administration/monitoring feature; it aggregates cron job status (admin-facing)
and has no content or access role. Note: gate the dashboard to admins. View the cron summary.
