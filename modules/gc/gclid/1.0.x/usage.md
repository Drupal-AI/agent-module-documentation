GCLID captures the Google Click ID from the URL and auto-fills it into form fields.

---

GCLID captures the Google Click ID (GCLID) — the ad-click identifier Google Ads appends to landing-page URLs — stores it (e.g. in a cookie/session), and auto-populates configured form fields with it, so conversions submitted through Drupal forms can be tied back to the originating ad click for offline conversion tracking.

It stores a marketing identifier tied to the visitor; handle per your privacy policy/consent. Administration is gated by `administer gclid settings`. Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
