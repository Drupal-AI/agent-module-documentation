Structured site-health diagnostics with trend history and machine-readable output.

---

Site Doctor provides site diagnosis over time — for humans, CI, and agents — running structured diagnostic checks, keeping trend history, and emitting machine-readable output, so site health can be tracked over time and consumed by CI pipelines or automated agents (not just a one-shot status page). Depends on core `user`; supports Drupal 11.
