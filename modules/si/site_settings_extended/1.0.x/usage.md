Site Settings Extended improves upon the Site Settings module.

---

Site Settings Extended **improves the Site Settings module** — adding Inline Entity Form and Field Group
support so site-wide settings (arbitrary configuration-like content fields) can be edited in richer, grouped
forms. It depends on the Site Settings, Inline Entity Form and Field Group modules, in the Custom package.

Use it for richer site-wide settings editing. It is a site-building/admin feature; site settings are edited by
privileged users (Site Settings' own permissions apply) and it has no access-control role of its own. Configure
the extended settings forms.
