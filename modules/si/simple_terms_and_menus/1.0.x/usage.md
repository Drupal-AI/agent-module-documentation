Simple Terms and Menus does cleanup on the menu and taxonomy forms.

---

Simple Terms and Menus **cleans up the taxonomy-term and menu-link forms** — simplifying/streamlining
those admin forms (hiding clutter, adding conveniences) to make managing terms and menus easier. It provides
its own permissions, in the Other package.

Use it to simplify term/menu editing. It is a content-editing/administration feature affecting those forms; it
has no content or access role beyond its permission. Enable it to streamline the forms.
