Simple Sitemap DiWoo produces a DiWoo-compliant sitemap for Dutch government transparency.

---

Simple Sitemap DiWoo generates a Simple Sitemap variant for DiWoo (Digitale Wet open overheid) — the Dutch Open Government Act's digital-publication requirements — so Dutch government sites can publish a DiWoo-compliant sitemap of their open-government documents for the required indexing/harvesting.

It's a sitemap/compliance module with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `simple_sitemap` and core `token`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
