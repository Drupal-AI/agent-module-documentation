Simple Sitemap Pagination URL customizes paginated sitemap file URLs.

---

Simple Sitemap Pagination URL provides custom sitemap URLs with pagination in the filename — adjusting how Simple Sitemap names/serves its paginated sitemap files, useful when a specific URL/filename structure is required for the sitemap index and its pages.

It's a sitemap-configuration enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `simple_sitemap`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
