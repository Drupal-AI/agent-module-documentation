Simple Open Hours provides a field to display open hours.

---

Simple Open Hours provides a **field to define and display opening hours** — storing per-day open/close
times and showing whether a place is currently open, using core Datetime and the Time Field module. It is in the
Field types package.

Use it to add opening hours to content. It is a fields/content-display feature; hours are admin/editor content
and it has no access-control role. Add the open-hours field.
