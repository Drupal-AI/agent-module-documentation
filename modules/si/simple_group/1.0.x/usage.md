Create and manage groups of users/content, simply.

---

Simple Groups lets you create and manage groups, simply — a lightweight grouping mechanism for organising users (and/or content) into groups, as a simpler alternative to the full Group module for basic membership needs. Depends on core `field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
