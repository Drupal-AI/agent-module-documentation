Gutenberg Extra adds a text filter that post-processes Gutenberg block output.

---

Gutenberg Extra adds a text-format filter that post-processes the output of the Gutenberg editor — refining or transforming the rendered block markup produced by Gutenberg-authored content. It's a small enhancement to the Gutenberg editing/display pipeline.

It's a filter/display enhancement with no content or access role of its own; ensure the filter is enabled on the relevant text formats. Depends on `gutenberg` and core `filter`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
