Assess compliance with the 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act (IDEA).

---

Digital.gov Site Scanner (idealyzer) assesses compliance with the 21st Century Integrated Digital Experience Act — scanning the site against the IDEA Act's digital-experience criteria (accessibility, mobile-friendliness, HTTPS, etc.) and reporting where it falls short, so US government/agency sites can track and improve compliance. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
