# Configuration

Digital Climate Strike Popup has a single, simple settings page where you choose the
popup widget to show visitors.

## Open the settings form

1. Log in as an administrator (a user who can administer site configuration).
2. Go to **Configuration → System → Digital Climate Strike Popup Settings**, or
   navigate directly to `/admin/config/dcs_popup/settings`.

## Choose the widget

- **Widget** — pick the Digital Climate Strike widget/style you want to display.
  This determines the popup or banner visitors see.

## Save

Click **Save configuration**. The chosen widget is shown to visitors on the front
end. Load a front-end page (as an anonymous visitor or in a private window) to
confirm the popup appears as expected.
