A custom field for embedding a Typeform form.

---

Typeform Field provides a custom field 'Typeform' to embed a form — so an editor can attach a Typeform (by its URL/ID) to content via a field and have the interactive Typeform embed render on the page, for surveys, quizzes and lead capture. Supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
