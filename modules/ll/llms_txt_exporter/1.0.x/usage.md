LLMs txt Exporter provides a dynamic llms.txt file based on site content.

---

LLMs txt Exporter **provides a dynamic llms.txt file** — generating the `llms.txt` guide-for-LLMs from your
Drupal content (the exporter approach, no AI), so LLM crawlers get a structured map of your site. It provides its
own permissions, in the SEO package.

Use it to publish a content-derived llms.txt. It is an SEO feature. Data-handling note: the `llms.txt` is
**published for LLM crawlers** and lists your content — include only content you intend to be publicly
discoverable, and review what's exposed. It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the llms.txt
export.
