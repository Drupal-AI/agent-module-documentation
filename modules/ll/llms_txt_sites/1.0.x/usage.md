LLMs.txt Sites integrates llms.txt sections with sites.

---

LLMs.txt Sites **integrates llms.txt with the Sites/Group multi-site structure** — so an `llms.txt` (the
convention for guiding AI crawlers/LLMs) can be composed per site/section in a multi-site setup, tying together
LLMs.txt, Sites, Group and form-decorator pieces. It depends on those modules.

Use it for per-site llms.txt in a Sites/Group setup. It is an SEO/AI-discoverability feature; llms.txt reflects
site content (which follows access) and it has no access-control role. Configure the per-site llms.txt.
