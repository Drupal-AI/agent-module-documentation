A monitoring web-service integration.

---

WSolution is a monitoring webservice integration — connecting Drupal to the WSolution monitoring service so site health/availability data can be reported to or retrieved from that platform.

The WSolution service credentials are admin-configured; store them securely (env-backed). Depends on `vendor_stream_wrapper`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
