Mail Denylist maintains a denylist to prevent mail delivery to blocked addresses/domains.

---

Mail Denylist **manages a denylist to prevent mail delivery** — blocking outbound email to configured
addresses/domains, useful to stop mail to known-bad/abusive recipients or to prevent accidental sends (e.g. to
production addresses from staging). It provides its own permissions.

Use it to block mail to certain recipients. It is a mail/administration feature; it filters outbound delivery and
has no access-control role beyond its permission. Note: it can also serve as a safety net (block all external mail
on non-production). Configure the mail denylist.
