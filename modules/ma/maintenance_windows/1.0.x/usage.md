Maintenance Windows automates entering/exiting maintenance mode on a schedule.

---

Maintenance Windows lets administrators schedule maintenance windows so the site automatically enters and exits maintenance mode at configured times — useful for planned downtime, deploys, or recurring maintenance without manual toggling.

Administration is gated by `administer maintenance_window configuration`. It's an operations utility with no content role. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
