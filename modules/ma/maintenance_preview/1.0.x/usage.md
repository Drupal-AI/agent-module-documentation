Maintenance Preview enables the maintenance page to be viewed without permission.

---

Maintenance Preview lets you **preview the maintenance page without enabling maintenance mode** — so you
can design/test the "site under maintenance" page without taking the whole site offline. It provides its own
permissions.

Use it to preview the maintenance page during development. It is an administration/development aid; it renders
the maintenance page on demand and does not change who can access the site during actual maintenance (core's
maintenance-mode access is unchanged). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Preview the
maintenance page.
