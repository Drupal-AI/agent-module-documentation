Integrate with the Make.as newsletter service (subscribe users).

---

Make Integration integrates with the Make newsletter service — fetching lists, managing a signup form, and subscribing users to a Make.as newsletter — so site visitors can subscribe and their details flow to Make.

The Make API credentials are admin-configured and should be stored securely (env-backed), never committed. Depends on core `field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
