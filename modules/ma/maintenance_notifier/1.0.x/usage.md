Maintenance Notifier sends an email when the site enters maintenance.

---

Maintenance Notifier **sends an email when the site enters (or leaves) maintenance mode** — notifying
configured recipients so operators know when the site is offline for maintenance. It depends on core Token, User
and System, in the Administration package.

Use it to alert operators on maintenance-mode changes. It is an operations feature; it emails configured
recipients (keep to operator addresses) and has no content or access role. Configure the notification recipients
and template.
