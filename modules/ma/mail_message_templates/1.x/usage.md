Mail Message Templates provides email message templates as configuration, letting emails be defined and managed as config entities.

---

Managing the site's outgoing emails — subjects and bodies — as configuration rather than scattered in code eases customisation and translation. Mail Message Templates provides email templates as config entities. It is a content/config tool. The templates likely support tokens; the consideration is what tokens resolve into an email (an email template should not surface sensitive data via a careless token), and that email content is subject to the usual handling. Because templates are config, they travel with a config export. Confirm the templates and their tokens output only intended content.
