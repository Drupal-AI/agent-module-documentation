Brid.TV integrates the Brid.TV video service.

---

Brid.TV **integrates the Brid.TV video platform** — providing a media source/handling so Brid.TV-hosted
videos can be embedded and managed as Drupal media. It depends on core Media, provides its own permissions.

Use it to embed Brid.TV videos. It is a media/integration feature. Security/data handling: it connects to the
**Brid.TV service/API** — store any **API credentials** as secrets over HTTPS, and video/player assets load from
**Brid.TV** (third-party embed). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the Brid.TV
integration.
