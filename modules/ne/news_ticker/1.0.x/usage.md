News Ticker provides a scrolling news-ticker block.

---

News Ticker **provides a news-ticker block** — a scrolling ticker showing headlines/announcements (from
configured items or content) in a block, for highlighting timely news. It provides its own permissions.

Use it to display a news ticker. It is a content-display feature; ticker content is admin/editor-managed and it has
no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the news-ticker block.
