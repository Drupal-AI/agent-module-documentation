Provides forex trading-journal functionality.

---

Forex Journal provides forex journal related functionality — a structured way to record and review foreign-exchange trades (entries, dates, outcomes) as content, so a trader can keep a trading journal on the site. Depends on core `field`, `text`, `datetime`, and `options`; supports Drupal 9.3+, 10, and 11.
