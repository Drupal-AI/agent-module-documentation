Xymatic provides Xymatic video integration.

---

Xymatic integrates **Xymatic video** with Drupal — letting Xymatic-hosted videos be embedded/managed as
Drupal media, with an `xymatic_migrate` submodule for migrating video data. It depends on core Media, in the
Media package.

Use it to use Xymatic videos as media. It is a media/integration feature; videos are hosted/served by
**Xymatic** and embedded (third-party content), media access follows core media/file access, and it has no
access-control role. Configure the Xymatic integration.
