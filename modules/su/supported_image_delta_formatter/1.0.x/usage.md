Display specific deltas of a multi-value Supported Image field.

---

Supported Image Delta Formatter provides a supported-image field formatter that displays one or more specific deltas (values) of a multi-value Supported Image field — so a display can show, e.g., only the first or a chosen image from a multi-value field. Depends on `image_delta_formatter` and `supported_image`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
