IXM Blocks Hero is the full-width introduction block at the top of a page.

---

The hero is the component with the most influence on how a page performs in both senses. It is what a visitor sees first, and its image is almost always the page's largest contentful paint — so the responsive image configuration on this block matters more than on any other, and preloading that image is often the single highest-value performance change available on a landing page.

The other recurring problem is contrast. Heading text over a photograph is legible over one image and illegible over the next, and the image is the thing editors change. An overlay, a scrim or a constrained text area is what makes it reliable; leaving legibility to whoever picks the photograph is how a page becomes unreadable in production without anyone changing the design.

Both of those are properties of heroes generally rather than of this implementation, and both are worth checking against whatever this block actually renders before it goes on a template used site-wide.
