Number In Word converts a number into its word-string representation.

---

Number In Word **converts numbers into word strings** — turning a numeric value into its spelled-out
representation (e.g. 123 → "one hundred twenty-three"), for invoices/amounts/accessibility. It works across core
9–11.

Use it to spell out numbers. It is a content/utility feature; it transforms number display and has no access-control
role. Use it to render numbers as words.
