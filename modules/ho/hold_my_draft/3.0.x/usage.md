Hold My Draft allows a hold of the latest revision and editing.

---

Hold My Draft lets editors **place a "hold" on a node's latest revision (draft)** — locking further editing
of the draft until it is released, so a draft can be frozen (e.g. pending review/approval) without publishing.
It depends on core Node and Content Moderation, provides its own permissions, in the Custom package.

Use it to freeze drafts pending review. It is an editorial-workflow feature; the hold and who can place/release
it are governed by its permissions (grant appropriately), and it works alongside Content Moderation. It has no
broad access-control role beyond its permissions. Configure the hold behaviour.
