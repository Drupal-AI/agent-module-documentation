Home Redirect Language redirects visitors landing on the homepage to their language.

---

Home Redirect Language **redirects visitors landing on the homepage to their language's homepage** — so a
visitor hitting `/` is sent to the localized front page for their (detected/preferred) language on a
multilingual site. It provides its own permissions, in the Multilingual package.

Use it for language-aware homepage redirects. It is a multilingual feature affecting the homepage redirect;
it has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure the redirect behaviour.
