Hover Card displays configurable user information in an elegant popup card on hover.

---

Hover Card **shows a popup card with user information when a visitor hovers over a username** — a
configurable card (name, picture, selected fields) that appears on hover, in the User package.

Use it to surface user info on hover. It is a user-engagement/display feature. **Privacy note:** the card shows
**user profile data**, so configure it to display **only fields that are safe to reveal** to the viewers who
will see the card — do not expose email addresses or other sensitive profile fields to unauthorized users
(field visibility should respect who can see the data). Its permission gates who sees the card. Configure the
card fields.
