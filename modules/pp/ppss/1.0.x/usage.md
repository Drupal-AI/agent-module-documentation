PPSS provides a simple payment/sales block integrating payment gateways.

---

Platform of Payments and Sales Simple (PPSS) allows users to pay for service purchases with payment gateways in a simple, affordable way — providing a configurable payment button/block so small sites can accept payments without a full commerce stack.

Payment gateway credentials should be stored securely (env-backed). Permissions cover viewing the button (`view ppss button`) and administration (`administer ppss`). Depends on core `block`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
