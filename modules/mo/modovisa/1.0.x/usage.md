Modovisa injects the Modovisa tracking snippet and can send Commerce events.

---

Modovisa adds the Modovisa tracking snippet to a Drupal site, with optional Drupal Commerce integration — so conversions and events can be tracked via Modovisa, including e-commerce events when Commerce is present. It's a marketing/analytics integration.

It loads a third-party tracking snippet (privacy/consent). Administration is gated by `administer modovisa`. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
