Monsido Tools integrates the Monsido platform (accessibility, quality assurance, SEO) into a Drupal site.

---

Monsido Tools integrates Monsido — a web-governance SaaS covering accessibility, quality assurance,
SEO and policy compliance — into a Drupal site, typically by embedding Monsido's script and providing
configuration to connect the site to a Monsido account. It is configured at `monsido.admin_settings` and
is in the Accessibility package.

Use it on sites monitored by Monsido for accessibility/quality auditing. It embeds a third-party
JavaScript/agent, so consider: external script loading (privacy/consent and CSP implications) and that
Monsido crawls/analyzes the site. It is an integration/accessibility-governance feature; configure the
Monsido account connection and where the script loads.
