An event-sourced commerce suite for Drupal 11+ with dual (Twig + JSON:API) rendering.

---

MonkeysCommerce is a commerce suite for Drupal 11+ — event-sourced orders, dual rendering (Twig + JSON API), and an extensible architecture — providing the base (`mkc_core`) on which the catalog, cart, order, checkout, payment, pricing, shipping and accelerator submodules build, for both traditional and headless stores.

It's a large commerce framework; store payment/API credentials securely (env-backed). Depends on `mkc_core`; supports Drupal 11.3+ and 12.
